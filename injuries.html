How injury/minutes data should be captured (method, not values)

  • Pull the official NBA injury report for each slate date.
  • For each injured player, record: status (out / questionable / probable), expected minutes restriction, and replacement player.
  • Estimate production impact (pts/reb/ast) for the injured player and their likely replacement, sourced from official NBA.com player stats.
  • Feed the adjusted minutes into player-prop signals and game-flow analysis before emitting picks.
  • Every value must link to an official source; blank + unverified otherwise.
