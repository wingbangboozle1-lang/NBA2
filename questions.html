Discovery

How to discover missing information

  • Betting lines/odds: evaluate licensed sportsbook data providers (official league data partners where available); document cost, update frequency, and terms.
  • Full schedule: pull NBA.com/schedule and team schedule pages (NBA.com/teams links each team to its schedule).
  • Minutes/production: use NBA.com/stats (official) for player minutes and production; verify each value against the official source.
  • Results/back-test: use official NBA game results (NBA.com) to back-test strategies and compute correlations.
  • Injury accuracy: cross-check the official NBA CDN injury PDF against each team’s official site; flag discrepancies in Flagged for Review.
  • Each discovery item: record the source link, date captured, and whether the value is verified or blank/unverified.
Decision rule: No betting decision or real-money recommendation should be made until questions 1–8 above are answered with verified data. Until then, all wagering values remain blank and flagged unverified.
