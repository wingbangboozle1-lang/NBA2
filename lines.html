Those originate from licensed sportsbooks (e.g., DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars), which are not official NBA sources and were not accessible. Line-origination method (framework, not values)

  • Start from official NBA.com team/player stats and schedule as the verified baseline.
  • Derive an implied line from team efficiency, pace, and recent form (skill-based signal, not luck).
  • Adjust for injuries and rotation minutes (tracked on Injuries & Game Flow page).
  • Compare originated line to sportsbook market; bet only where a validated edge exists and the strategy passes validation gates.
  • Every numeric line value must carry a source link before it is shown; otherwise it stays blank + unverified.
