Strategies
Signal-generation strategies for single bets, parlays, and player-prop bet combos. Treated as a leaderboard competition that rewards top-performing strategies. Each strategy must pass validation gates before it is used to generate picks.
Design principle: remove luck from the equation; build strategies that are selectively skill-based. Signals must come from verified official NBA data (schedule, standings, player/team stats) plus a sportsbook market comparison. Where a value cannot be confirmed from an official source, it is left blank and flagged unverified.

How a strategy becomes a pick

  1. Originate a line for a market using verified official NBA data as baseline.
  2. Check the strategy against every validation gate (see Validation Gates page).
  3. Determine correlation values between legs (see Correlation Values page) for parlays / prop combos.
  4. Adjust for injuries and rotation minutes (see Injuries & Game Flow page).
  5. If all gates pass and a validated edge exists vs the sportsbook market, emit a pick for the upcoming slate (max 3 days ahead).
  6. Record the pick, its source links, and its unverified/verified status on the Picks page.
