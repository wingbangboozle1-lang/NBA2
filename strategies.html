Strategies

Originated NBA lines and betting strategies, organized by bet type, with validation gates and correlation tracking. All wagering values are left blank and flagged unverified where no official NBA source exists.

Approach

Signal-generation approach

Strategies are treated as a leaderboard competition that rewards top-performing strategies. Each strategy generates a signal (side + confidence), is back-tested against available results, must pass validation gates, and is tracked for correlation with other strategies so the portfolio does not double-count the same edge. Luck is removed by requiring selective, skill-based signals rather than blanket coverage.
