How to read a verdict

SUPERSEDED (+n) — same idea, with the number of games that have elapsed. MISSING — the official source has the row and the publication does not. FAIL — the publication contradicts the official source, or an operational claim fails. UNVERIFIABLE — cannot be checked from here; the reason is in the note.
