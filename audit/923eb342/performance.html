The one contradiction worth resolving first

The walk-forward backtest reports 0.6735 against-the-spread accuracy out of sample on 49 games, while the settled-ledger log-loss is 0.7058 against a coin flip's 0.6931. A model can be right about the side and badly calibrated about the probability — but the two surfaces use different samples, different settlement rules and different baselines, and no published page reconciles them. Until one does, neither number should be quoted alone.
