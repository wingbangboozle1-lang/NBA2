Executive summary

The system is honestly built and honestly labelled, and it is not currently doing the job it is supposed to do. The three sentences below are the whole summary.

1 · The pipeline is trustworthy

Provenance is enforced by code, not by discipline: an unsourced value raises, an unexplained blank raises, and CI rebuilds every published number from committed snapshots. This is rarer than it sounds and it is the asset.

2 · The operation is not running

Ten consecutive scheduled refreshes failed. The capture half works; the publish half does not. Result rows stopped at 2026-11-14 and the injury archive at 11/17, while 17 games went final.

3 · There is no edge, and one measurement says the model is worse than a coin

No price ⇒ no edge by construction. Independently, out-of-sample log-loss is worse than 0.6931. Until those are reconciled or a price exists, nothing here is a bet.

What I recommend, in order

  1. Fix publication before adding anything. A pipeline that cannot publish makes every other improvement unobservable. Start with SG-923-01 (the gate that freezes one week's numbers) and Q-923-01 (read the runner log).
  2. Reconcile the two skill measurements. 67.3% against the spread and a log-loss worse than a coin cannot both describe the same model without an explanation. That explanation is worth more than any new strategy.
  3. Stand up the archive layer, not the strategy layer. Player logs at 10.3% coverage and injury reports that stop at 11/17 are data-engineering problems with data-engineering answers (a 15-minute index cron). Strategy work on top of them is guesswork.
  4. Then resume originating lines, sealed before tip, with the disagreement published as the headline and the blend beneath it.
