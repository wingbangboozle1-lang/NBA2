Line origination (v1.0)

predicted home margin = (NRtg/A_home − NRtg/A_away) + HCA − injury_home + injury_away + fatigue terms
predicted total       = league mean + scoring-lean terms − 0.35 × combined injury impact

Inputs: schedule-adjusted net ratings (Basketball-Reference, trusted secondary — flagged per row), official injury statuses (official PDF), verified rest. Parameters (HCA 2.0, tiers 4.0/2.0/0.8, fatigue −0.6/−1.1…) are documented assumptions pending calibration (Q-3).

Removing luck — three mechanisms

  1. Expected-record ledger: luck component = actual wins − Σ model win probability. Current extreme: PHI 10-3 with expected 5.8 → +4.2 luck.
  2. Process Rating = 0.40·z(shrunk ROI) + 0.35·z(CLV) + 0.15·z(gates) − 0.10·z(drawdown); raw ROI is displayed but labeled luck-contaminated. CLV is the accepted skill measure — "closing line value accumulates with skill" (source).
  3. Conviction gating: play only where |our margin| ≥ 4 or total lean ≥ 5 pts — selectively skill-based by construction.

Prop-combo rules

  • Projection = verified season baseline + 0.35 × opponent defense adjustment; bet-able at ±1.5-pt buffer around our line.
  • Combos must follow documented correlation direction; big-favorite star props banned (blowout minutes); game-under + props banned.
