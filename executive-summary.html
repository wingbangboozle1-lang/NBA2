Executive Summary

Key details of the NBA2 lines-origination, signal, and picks platform.

Objective

Originate NBA lines, develop selectively skill-based signal strategies (single bets, parlays, player-prop combos), validate every strategy through gates, measure correlations, remove luck, and generate picks for every upcoming slate up to 3 days in advance — with injury-driven rotation and game-flow analysis.

What the system does

  • Originates lines from verified inputs (team win-loss records, home/away, official injury statuses) using a transparent, auditable model. Lines are labeled Model.
  • Generates picks for the full 3-day slate, up to 3 days ahead, using originated lines.
  • Validates every strategy through five gates (data integrity, edge, correlation, sample size, luck/skill) before a signal is eligible.
  • Measures correlations and enforces a parlay correlation gate so combos use independent legs.
  • Tracks injuries and their impact on rotation minutes and production, producing game-flow analysis.

Verification posture

All factual data — schedule, records, and injuries — is verified against NBA.com and linked per row. The system deliberately does not infer unverified values: where a value cannot be confirmed from an official source, it is left blank and flagged (e.g., PPG/OPPG for 28 of 30 teams; player-prop values; historical correlations). Model outputs are never presented as official.

Current limitations (all flagged, none blocking): totals use an unverified league-average baseline; injury-impact weights are model estimates pending player-level usage data; no games have been tracked to completion, so all strategies remain provisional.
