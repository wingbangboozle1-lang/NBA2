Executive Summary

Important project details, methodology, verification stance, and current status at a glance.

Objective

Objective

Originate NBA betting lines and generate useful betting signals (single bets, parlays, player-prop combos). Treat strategies as a leaderboard competition with rewards for top performers. Remove luck by requiring selectively skill-based signals that pass validation gates. Generate picks for every upcoming slate up to 3 days in advance using originated lines. Track injuries and their effect on rotation minutes and production, with game-flow analysis.

Verification stance

Verification stance

  • Every table row that states a value carries a source link to an official, verified, trusted site (NBA.com or NBA-owned CDN).
  • If a value cannot be confirmed from an official source, it is left blank and flagged unverified.
  • Nothing is inferred or filled in. No odds, lines, correlations, minutes, or projections are invented.
  • Betting lines and odds are not published by the NBA — all wagering values are blank / unverified pending a licensed sportsbook data source.
  • Source-data anomalies in the official injury PDF are reproduced verbatim and flagged (see Flagged for Review).
  • No manual input was required; all entries were researched and written by this session.
