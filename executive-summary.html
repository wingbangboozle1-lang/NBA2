Executive Summary
Important details about the NBA2 originated-lines system, current status, and what must happen before any betting decision is reasonably made.

What this system is

NBA2 collects, organizes, and analyzes originated NBA lines and strategy signals for single bets, parlays, and player-prop combinations. It operates as a leaderboard competition that rewards top-performing strategies. Every strategy must pass validation gates, correlation values are determined, and the design removes luck so strategies are selectively skill-based. Picks are generated for every upcoming slate up to a maximum of 3 days in advance using originated lines. Injury impact on rotation minutes and production, plus game-flow analysis, are tracked.

Key findings

  • Verified baseline exists. Official NBA.com provides schedule, game counts, team records (as displayed), and player/team stat leaders. These are the only inputs safe to use.
  • No official betting lines. NBA.com does not publish spreads, moneylines, totals, or player props. All originated-line and odds values are blank + unverified.
  • Injury report inaccessible. The official NBA injuries page did not return content; all injury/minutes/production values are blank + unverified.
  • Standings table not extracted. The standings page exists but its table is JavaScript-rendered and was not captured; team W/L records are blank + unverified except where shown on the games page.
  • Zero validated strategies or picks. Until lines and injuries are sourced from verified providers, no strategy can pass validation gates and no pick can be emitted.

What must happen before any decision

  1. Integrate an accessible sportsbook market source and confirm every line/odds value against it (or leave blank + unverified).
  2. Integrate an accessible official injury-report source and confirm injury/minutes/production values.
  3. Extract the 2026-27 standings table (W/L per team) from an official source.
  4. Confirm Nov 14 and Nov 15 matchups from an official source.
  5. Build and validate strategies against all gates (GATE-01–GATE-07) and correlation values.
  6. Emit picks only for slates within 3 days, only from strategies that pass all gates.
  7. Review the Questions & Answers and Suggestions pages before acting.
