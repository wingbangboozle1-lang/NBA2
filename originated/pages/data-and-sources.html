A note on the unweighted mean

Averaging PPG across the 30 teams without weighting by games played does not satisfy the accounting identity, because teams have played between 10 and 13 games. The unweighted means are 113.41 vs 113.54 — a 0.13 gap that looks like a data error but is an artefact. All league averages in this build are games-weighted.
