S09 — Same-Game Parlay: correlation mispricing parlay

Books price same-game parlays by multiplying leg probabilities as if the legs were independent. When two legs from one game are POSITIVELY correlated, the true joint probability is higher than the product, so the independent price is generous. When they are NEGATIVELY correlated the independent price is a trap.
