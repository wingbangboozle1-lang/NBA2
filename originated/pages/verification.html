Verification

How every number on this site was checked, and what is still unchecked.

1. The rule

Every table row must carry a source link to an official site. If a value cannot be confirmed from

an official source, it is left blank and flagged as unverified. No value is inferred or filled in.

Enforcement is structural, not procedural:

  • Every row in data/*.json carries a src field naming an entry in data/sources.json.
  • data/sources.json holds the URL, the tier and the retrieval date for each source.
  • Unverifiable numeric fields are stored as null, not zero and not an estimate.
  • The site renders null as — UNVERIFIED — and lists every such cell on the Flagged page.
  • Model output is never presented as data; it is labelled MODEL OUTPUT and its formula is published

    • in Line Origination.

3. Manual verification performed

Official NBA schedule — nba.com/schedule

All 23 games were read row by row from the server-rendered page: date, tip time (ET), teams, venue, city, broadcaster and Cup group. The games-per-day counts were cross-checked against the page's own headers ("9 Games", "5 Games", "9 Games"). Each game additionally has its own official NBA.com game page linked from the Slates table — open any of them to confirm a row directly.

Official NBA injury report — PDF

All 7 pages transcribed row by row: 81 player rows plus 10 NOT YET SUBMITTED team rows. Player names, statuses and reason strings are reproduced verbatim, including the report's own punctuation (e.g. "Injury/Illness - Left Great Toe - Sprain").

Two teams that play on 13 Nov — Milwaukee and New York — have no player rows in this edition. That is the report as published, not a transcription gap, and it is flagged (F-INJ-01).

Team records — cross-verified between two independent sources

W-L for all 30 teams was taken from the T1 NBA.com game rail and compared row by row against the T2 ESPN standings. 30 of 30 match. The 18 teams playing on 13 Nov were independently re-read from the ESPN scoreboard as a third check: 18 of 18 agree.

Team scoring splits — T2 only

PPG, OPP PPG, DIFF, home/away/div/conf splits, streak and last-10 come from ESPN. These are not from an official NBA property, because stats.nba.com is unreachable from this build environment. Every one of these cells is therefore flagged "T2 — trusted, not official" on the site.

5. How to re-verify any figure yourself

  1. Open the page showing the figure.
  2. Follow the source link in the row (or the page's source panel).
  3. For model output, open the formula in Line Origination and re-run

    4. node engine/compute.js — the simulator is seeded, so you will get the identical numbers.

If a figure disagrees with its source, it is a bug. Please log it in COORDINATION.md §6.
