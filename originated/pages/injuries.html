Three indices, and why they are separate

A G League two-way assignment removes a player who was not in the NBA rotation. Counting those rows as rotation losses is the easiest way to get this wrong. They are tracked separately and excluded from the index that drives the betting signal.

How to unblock this

  1. Restore stats.nba.com access, or obtain an official NBA Stats API key → leaguedashplayerstats gives season minutes and usage.
  2. vacated minutes = Σ (minutes of players listed Out or Doubtful)
  3. Redistribute by each remaining rotation player's usage rate.
  4. Only then calibrate injuryK — after at least one full season of that data.
