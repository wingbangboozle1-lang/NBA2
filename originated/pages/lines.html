The derived home-court advantage is only +0.53 points. It comes from 89 home wins against 84 home losses in undefined split games — a standard error of roughly ±3.8 percentage points, so this estimate is unstable, and it is the largest single source of error in the originated spreads. The panel at the bottom re-prices the whole board at +0.5 / +1.5 / +2.5 so the effect is visible rather than hidden.
