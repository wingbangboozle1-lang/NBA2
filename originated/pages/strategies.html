S01 — Pythagorean Regression (Luck Fade) single

Thesis. A team whose actual win% sits far above its Pythagorean win% has been winning close games it did not really dominate; the reverse is true for a team underperforming its scoring profile. Fade the lucky, back the unlucky.

Residual signal: Pythagorean win% vs actual win% (not used by the originated line).

Fires when: |luck differential| >= 0.15

Where: luckDiff = home.luck - away.luck; adjustment is applied against the luckier team.

Inputs: ppg, oppPpg, wins, losses — T2 (S04); W-L cross-checked T1 (S02)

λ = 12 — Margin points per 1.00 of luck differential. ASSUMPTION

S02 — Recent-Form vs Season Divergence single

Thesis. When a team's last-10 record disagrees materially with its full-season record, trust the larger sample: fade the hot streak, back the slump.

Residual signal: Last-10 win% vs season win% (not used by the originated line).

Fires when: |L10 win% - season win%| differential >= 0.20

Where: l10Diff = (home.L10% - home.season%) - (away.L10% - away.season%).

Inputs: last10, wins, losses — T2 (S04)

λ = 10 — Margin points per 1.00 of L10-vs-season differential. ASSUMPTION

S03 — Venue-Split Divergence single

Thesis. Some teams are materially better or worse at home than their overall net rating implies, and likewise on the road. Compare the home team's home record with the away team's road record.

Residual signal: Home/away split records (the line uses a league-average home-court term only).

Fires when: |home-team home win% - away-team road win%| >= 0.30, with >= 5 games in each split

Where: venueDiff = homeTeam.homeWinPct - awayTeam.roadWinPct.

Inputs: home, away — T2 (S04)

λ = 6 — Margin points per 1.00 of venue-split differential. ASSUMPTION

S04 — Availability Fade (official injury report) single

Thesis. Teams carrying a heavier unavailability load are weaker than their season-to-date net rating implies, because that net rating was earned with healthier rotations.

Residual signal: Weighted unavailability index built from the official NBA injury report. Explicitly NOT applied to the published line.

Fires when: |rotation-availability index differential| >= 1.00

Where: rotationDiff = home.rotationIndex - away.rotationIndex. Weights: Out 1.00, Doubtful 0.75, Questionable 0.40, Probable 0.15, Available 0.00. G League two-way and G League assignment rows are EXCLUDED, because those players were not in the NBA rotation. NOT YET SUBMITTED is also excluded - it is an absence of information, not information.

Inputs: injury report status, reason category — T1 (S03)

λ = 0.35 — Margin points per 1.00 of unavailability index. UNCALIBRATED. ASSUMPTION

S05 — Emirates NBA Cup point-differential incentive single

Thesis. If the Cup group tiebreaker is point differential, teams have a documented incentive to keep scoring late in blowouts, biasing Cup games toward the over.

Residual signal: Whether a game is an Emirates NBA Cup group game (known from the official schedule).

Fires when: game is an Emirates NBA Cup group game

Where: Applied to the projected total only.

Inputs: competition — T1 (S01)

S06 — Extreme-Streak Mean Reversion single

Thesis. Very long losing (or winning) streaks are partly noise. Fade streaks of 6+ games in either direction.

Residual signal: Current win/loss streak length (not used by the originated line).

Fires when: either team on a streak of 6+ games

Where: signedStreak = +n for a W n streak, -n for an L n streak; adjustment favours the team on the longer losing streak.

Inputs: streak — T2 (S04)

λ = 0.12 — Margin points per game of streak beyond 5, applied in the mean-reverting direction. ASSUMPTION

S07 — Totals: Fade the Extremes single

Thesis. Projected totals at the extremes of the distribution regress: the highest-projected games tend to land lower than the raw scoring-environment maths implies, and the lowest higher.

Residual signal: Distance of the originated total from the league norm (the line itself, regressed back).

Fires when: |projected total - 2 x league PPG| >= 6 points

Where: totalVsNorm = projected total - 2 x league-average PPG.

Inputs: ppg, oppPpg — T2 (S04)

λ = 0.35 — Fraction of the league-norm gap removed from the projected total. ASSUMPTION

S08 — Cross-Game Parlay Builder (correlation-controlled) parlay

Thesis. Parlaying legs from different games keeps inter-leg correlation at ~0, so the fair parlay price is the product of the leg prices. Parlays then amplify per-leg edge multiplicatively - and amplify vig just as fast when there is no edge.

Residual signal: Independence across games (verified by construction, since games are disjoint).

Fires when: at least 2 qualifying legs, each from a different game

Inputs: single-leg model probabilities — derived

S09 — Same-Game Parlay: correlation mispricing parlay

Thesis. Books price same-game parlays by multiplying leg probabilities as if the legs were independent. When two legs from one game are POSITIVELY correlated, the true joint probability is higher than the product, so the independent price is generous. When they are NEGATIVELY correlated the independent price is a trap.

Residual signal: Within-game correlation between market outcomes, measured by Monte-Carlo (not used anywhere else).

Fires when: |phi| >= 0.05 between the two legs

Inputs: simulated joint distribution of margin and total — derived
