The engine is ready

Strategy S10 (Player Prop Combos) is already wired into the validation-gate framework and currently sits at 5/8 gates, failing G2 (provenance), G5 and G6. It needs no code work — only a data source. Once Q-02 is answered:

  1. Pull per-player season minutes and usage from an official feed.
  2. Project minutes: base season minutes, boosted by the vacated minutes of Out/Doubtful teammates.
  3. Project production: projected minutes × per-minute rate, adjusted for opponent defensive rating.
  4. Originate the threshold, then price it against the market once Q-01 is answered.
  5. Only then re-run the gates.
