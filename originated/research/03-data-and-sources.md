# Data & Sources

Every number published on this site traces to one of five sources. Sources carry a **tier**:

| Tier | Meaning | Hosts here |
|---|---|---|
| **T1** | Official NBA property | `nba.com`, `ak-static.cms.nba.com` |
| **T2** | Trusted league partner / major rights holder | `espn.com`, `site.api.espn.com` |
| **T3** | Other aggregator | **never used for any published number** |

A row's `src` field in `data/*.json` points at an entry in
[`data/sources.json`](../data/sources.json).

---

## Source register

| ID | Tier | Source | URL | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S01 | T1 | NBA.com Schedule | https://www.nba.com/schedule | dates, tip times, venues, cities, broadcasters, Cup groups, games-per-day counts |
| S02 | T1 | NBA.com game page (schedule rail) | https://www.nba.com/game/nyk-vs-ind-0022600034 | win-loss records for all 30 teams, Cup group records, Cup group assignment |
| S03 | T1 | Official NBA Injury Report 11/13/26 01:00 PM | https://ak-static.cms.nba.com/referee/injury/Injury-Report_2026-11-13_01_00PM.pdf | player status, reason, game assignment, `NOT YET SUBMITTED` |
| S04 | T2 | ESPN NBA Standings 2026-27 | https://www.espn.com/nba/standings | PPG, OPP PPG, DIFF, home/away/div/conf splits, streak, last-10 |
| S05 | T2 | ESPN NBA Scoreboard | https://www.espn.com/nba/scoreboard | independent re-read of the 18 teams playing 13 Nov |

Every game also links its own official NBA.com game page, which is the T1 source for that
game's existence and both teams' records.

## Unreachable official endpoints

| Host | Attempted | Result | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| `stats.nba.com` | `leaguestandingsv3`, `scoreboardv3` | connection refused | no official per-player averages; no official pace / ORTG / DRTG |
| `cdn.nba.com` | `scheduleLeagueV2_1.json`, `todaysScoreboard_00.json` | HTTP 500 | substituted the server-rendered `www.nba.com/schedule` page |

## Cross-verification performed

| Check | Method | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **W‑L, all 30 teams** | T2 (ESPN standings) compared row-by-row with T1 (nba.com game rail covering 13–17 Nov, which includes every team) | **30 / 30 match** |
| **Points accounting identity** | games-weighted `mean(PPG)` must equal games-weighted `mean(OPP PPG)` | **PASS** — 113.43 vs 113.42 (difference is 1-decimal rounding in the source) |
| **Games-per-day counts** | row counts vs the "9 Games / 5 Games / 9 Games" headers on nba.com | **PASS** — 9 / 5 / 9 = 23 |
| **Fair-line self-consistency** | Monte-Carlo `P(home covers its own originated spread)` must be ≈ 0.500 | **PASS** — worst deviation 0.0055 across 23 games |
| **Simulator calibration** | realised margin SD ≈ 14.5, total SD ≈ 20.0, score correlation ≈ 0.31 | **PASS** |

### A note on the unweighted mean

Averaging PPG across the 30 teams **without weighting by games played** does not satisfy the
accounting identity, because teams have played between 10 and 13 games. The unweighted means are
113.41 vs 113.54 — a 0.13 gap that looks like a data error but is an artefact. All league
averages in this build are **games-weighted**.

## Known gaps in the source data

| Gap | Severity | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| No official per-player season feed | **high** | all player props blank (Q‑02) |
| No official pace / possessions data | medium | ratings are per-game, not per-100-possessions |
| No official schedule strength | medium | no opponent adjustment in the ratings; only mean-reversion shrinkage |
| No 12 Nov results collected | low | rest/back-to-back state is unknown for the 13 Nov games and is set to 0 |
| `stats.nba.com` unreachable | high | see Q‑02, Q‑05 |
| Injury report is a point-in-time snapshot | medium | teams re-file later the same day; anything dated 13 Nov must be re-pulled |
