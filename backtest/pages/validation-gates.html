What the failures mean

  • G-04 is DISPUTED. It passes its registered wording — the model's MAE beats an unshrunk raw point differential — but that baseline is a strawman: in week one an unshrunk differential reads anywhere from −66 to +31 points and is wrong by construction. Against a fair baseline (the same shrinkage, no home-court term) the model is 0.08 points worse. The honest reading: shrinkage does all the work, the home-court term adds nothing, and this gate is not evidence of skill.
  • G-01 FAILS. 167 graded games versus a 300-game floor.
  • G-07 PASSES. It was previously "at risk by construction" because the only snapshot was an as-of table that leaks the future. A leakage-tested walk-forward now exists.
  • G-02, G-05 (ROI half), G-06, G-09, G-10 stay BLOCKED. All five need prices or player-level inputs. They are not marked pass by proxy.
