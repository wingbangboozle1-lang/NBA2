Game flow & rest

The repository asserted that back-to-backs and travel depress performance. This page tests it on official dates and reports the strength of the evidence rather than the direction of the story.

What can be measured from official dates

  • Days of rest entering each game, derived only from the official schedule dates.
  • Back-to-back appearance (exactly 1 day of rest).
  • "3 games in 4 days" (this game plus two or more in the preceding three days).
  • Margin in each situation, and a permutation test between back-to-backs and rested teams.

Travel, altitude and motivation

These are the flow factors that appear on the other surfaces of this repo. They are not measured here, because doing so honestly needs per-team travel schedules and venue metadata that this snapshot does not carry in machine-readable form:

  • Travel distance — the schedule gives venues, not itineraries. Distance could be geocoded from the arena list; that is a defined next step, not a current result.
  • Altitude — Denver/Utah home games are identifiable from the venue string, but the effect needs a much larger sample than 148 games.
  • Motivation (Cup group standings, seeding, back-half of a road trip) — not quantified, and not claimed.
  • In-game flow (pace, runs, garbage time) — needs play-by-play, which this snapshot does not include. Every in-game flow column on the other site surfaces remains blank for this reason.
