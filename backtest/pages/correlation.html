Correlation, measured

The repository previously published correlations as structural placeholders. These are computed from official final scores, with the sample size beside every value.

Finding: the same-game parlay premise is not supported. Strategy S-04 assumed the "favourite covers AND the total goes over" legs were positively dependent so a same-game parlay would be underpriced. Measured: r(margin, total) = -0.0089 on 155 games, 95% CI -0.1663 to 0.149. The dependence is not detectable, so S-04's edge does not exist on this evidence.

Back-to-back minus rested: -2.81 points, permutation p = 0.38 — not statistically significant.

A negative back-to-back margin is the fatigue story. The sign here is negative, which is consistent with it, but the p-value says this sample cannot separate it from noise. The qualitative claim on earlier pages ("back-to-backs depress production") is therefore directionally consistent but unproven.
