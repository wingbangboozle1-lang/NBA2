Reference material (definitions, not data)

  • Wilson (1927) score interval — used for every hit-rate confidence interval.
  • Brier (1950) — the probability scoring rule used in gate G-03.
  • Fisher (1935) randomisation / permutation testing — used for every p-value here.
  • Holm (1979) — Bonferroni-style multiple-comparison control, referenced by gate G-06.
  • Bailey & López de Prado (2014) — deflated Sharpe ratio, the basis for the search penalty on the leaderboard.

These are method citations, not numeric sources. No number on this site was taken from them.
