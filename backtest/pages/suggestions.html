S-102Replace the four-rate total with a pace-and-efficiency model, or publish no total at all.Total MAE (15.78) is worse than a constant (15.43). A total that loses to a constant should not be published.Total MAE and bias on the same games.Removing the total removes the S-04 parlay test entirely.
