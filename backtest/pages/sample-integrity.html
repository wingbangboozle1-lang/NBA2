Sample & integrity

An accuracy number is worthless if the outcomes it was measured on are wrong. So the outcomes are checked first, and the checks are shown.

How the sample was captured

  • Source: nba.com/games date pages, read one day at a time.
  • 164 games across 22 dates (2026-10-20 → 2026-11-13).
  • Each game keeps its own nba.com/game/<slug>-<id> URL, so any row can be re-checked by hand.
  • Overtime is stored as a period count (4 = regulation), because two games in this window went to double overtime and a boolean would have silently flattened them.
Two dates could not be verified and are excluded, not guessed. The official pages for 2026-11-06 (9 games) and 2026-11-11 (10 games) rendered with no scores — the score elements were empty. Those 19 games are recorded as unverified and left out of every calculation on this site. See FLAG-BT-01.

Check 1 — do reconstructed records match the official post-game records?

Every nba.com game card prints both teams' W-L after that game. Rebuilding each team's record from final scores alone and comparing with those printed cards is an independent test of both the scores and the home/away assignment.

What this sample is not

  • Not complete. 22 dates, not the full season; the two excluded dates are listed above.
  • Not a market sample. No prices were captured, so nothing here is an ROI.
  • Not injury-adjusted. The results snapshot carries no player data.
  • Not point-in-time for injuries. Availability is ignored, which is a documented weakness of the model, not a hidden one.

Every row links its own source. Nothing here is betting advice, and no bet is placed by this repository. No odds feed is connected, so no ROI, edge or stake is published anywhere on this site.
