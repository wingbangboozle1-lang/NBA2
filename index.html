About this platform

A self-contained system that originates its own NBA lines, develops skill-based signal strategies, validates every strategy through gates, measures correlations, removes luck, and generates picks for every upcoming slate up to 3 days in advance — with injury-driven rotation and game-flow analysis.

Every factual value (schedule, records, injuries) is verified against NBA.com official sources and linked per row. Lines, projections, and picks are model-originated and labeled Model — they are not official sportsbook lines. Anything that cannot be confirmed from an official source is left blank and flagged as unverified on the Flagged for Review page.
