About NBA2

This site collects, organizes, and analyzes originated NBA lines and strategy signals for single bets, parlays, and player-prop combinations. It is treated as a leaderboard competition that rewards top-performing strategies. Each strategy must pass validation gates, correlation values are determined, and luck is removed so strategies are selectively skill-based.

Picks are generated for every upcoming slate of games up to a maximum of 3 days in advance using originated lines. Injury impact on rotation minutes and production, plus game-flow analysis, are tracked. All findings are presented in easy-to-read tables with source links.
