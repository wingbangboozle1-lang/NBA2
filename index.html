NBA2

Originated NBA lines, strategy signals, validation gates, and picks — built entirely from official NBA sources. Evidence first: no fabricated picks, lines, or performance claims.

This dashboard collects, organizes, and analyzes NBA betting signals. Strategies are treated as a leaderboard competition (rewards for top performers), cover single bets, parlays, and player-prop combos, and must pass validation gates before any pick is issued. Picks are generated for every upcoming slate up to 3 days in advance using our originated lines.

Research foundations (integrated)

What we can—and cannot—say

Available (official NBA)
Schedules, player/team statistics, and team-submitted injury reports. Each is a potential input, not a ready-made betting signal.
Not available here
No historical snapshots, market prices, archived injury updates, model estimates, validation results, or bankroll records.
Live model / odds feed
None in this repository. No picks, lines, or returns are published until sourced and validated.
Decision: do not issue a wager recommendation until calibrated probabilities, executable price/odds, uncertainty, and all required validation gates are available.
Candidate strategy families

Research backlog (hypotheses to test, not findings)

FamilySignal hypothesisMeasure / dependenceStatus
Game sides & totals Team/player performance features may help estimate game outcomes; compare model probability to a time-stamped market quote. Log loss, Brier score, calibration, closing-line value, net ROI after costs; estimate dependence by game/date. unverified · no model/data
Player props Role, minutes opportunity, opponent/context and player production may inform a distribution; actual rotation is uncertain. Distribution calibration and error by prop; conditional dependence across props and teammates. unverified · no prop feed
Parlays / prop combos Test joint probability directly; multiplying marginal probabilities is invalid when legs are dependent unless independence is justified. Joint calibration, correlation/covariance by pair and context, payout break-even, hold and net return. blocked · no joint sample
Injury / rotation response Model status changes and observed role/minutes distributions; do not mechanically transfer absent-player production to teammates. Minute and production distributions conditional on report timestamp/status; uncertainty and sample size. blocked · no archived reports
Validation gates

Evidence before leaderboard rewards (all required)

GateRequired evidenceFail conditionReference
1. Point-in-time integrity Immutable feature, injury, schedule and quote snapshots with event-time and ingestion-time; features available before decision cutoff. Leakage, missing timestamps, retrospective injury/status substitution. NBA injury reporting
2. Baselines & holdout Pre-registered baseline; chronological train/validation/test split; rolling-origin evaluation; no tuning on final holdout. Random leakage, post-hoc selection, insufficient coverage. NBA stats reference
3. Probability quality Proper scoring (Brier/log loss), reliability/calibration by probability bucket, confidence intervals and subgroup checks. Uncalibrated or unstable probabilities; any key metric omitted. NBA stats reference
4. Tradable economics Timestamped offered odds, rules/settlement, vig removal method, assumed execution/slippage and costs; compare ROI to break-even. Backtest uses unavailable prices or ignores hold/costs. Official schedule
5. Skill / luck control Large pre-specified sample; uncertainty intervals; bootstrap by slate/date; multiple-testing correction across candidate strategies. Winner selected from many variants without correction or uncertainty disclosure. NBA Stats
6. Correlation & combos Report pairwise and joint dependence on out-of-sample observations, with sample counts, intervals and context; estimate combo probabilities directly. Assume independent legs or present unsupported correlation as fact. NBA player stats
7. Prospective shadow run Freeze model; record every eligible prediction before outcome; compare against benchmark over a defined prospective window. Selective logging, missing losing picks, changed model without versioning. NBA schedule
8. Responsible limits Document jurisdiction, permitted data/market use, access controls, risk limits, and no-guarantee messaging before public wagering use. Unreviewed legal/data rights or unsafe promises. NBA official site

Passing gates is a governance threshold, not proof of future profitability. No gate has been evaluated for a strategy in this repo.

Verification stance

How data is verified

  • Every table row that states a value carries a source link to an official, verified, trusted site (NBA.com or NBA-owned CDN).
  • If a value cannot be confirmed from an official source, it is left blank and flagged unverified.
  • Nothing is inferred or filled in. No odds, lines, correlations, minutes, or projections are invented.
  • Betting lines and odds are not published by the NBA — all wagering values are blank / unverified pending a licensed sportsbook data source.
  • Source-data anomalies in the official injury PDF are reproduced verbatim and flagged (see Flagged for Review).
  • No manual input was required; all entries were researched and written by this session.
