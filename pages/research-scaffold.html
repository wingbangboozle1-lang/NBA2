01 / EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

What we can—and cannot—say

Available

The NBA publishes official schedules, player/team statistics, and team-submitted injury reports. Each is a potential input, not a ready-made betting signal.

Not available here

No historical snapshots, market prices, archived injury updates, model estimates, validation results, or bankroll records exist in this repository.

Recommendation

Begin with timestamped single-market forecasts and strict out-of-sample evaluation. Add combinations only after component and joint dependence are measured.

Decision: do not issue a wager recommendation until calibrated probabilities, executable price/odds, uncertainty, and all required gates are available.

05 / NEXT STEPS

Recommended sequence

  1. Establish licensed/authorized, timestamped historical inputs and a reproducible data dictionary.
  2. Choose the target market, market cutoff, settlement rules, jurisdiction, and baseline.
  3. Build one single-bet probability model and chronological backtest; publish calibration and uncertainty, not just win rate.
  4. Run prospective shadow predictions; only then test combo dependence and compare net performance.
  5. Gate a versioned leaderboard; keep failed, inconclusive, and retired strategies visible.
