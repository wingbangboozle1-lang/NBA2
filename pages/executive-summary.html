Executive Summary

High-level overview of the NBA2 originated-lines and strategy system, current state, findings, and recommendations.

System Objective

Collect, organize, and analyze NBA data to originate betting lines, generate skill-based strategy signals for single bets, parlays, and player-prop combos, validate every strategy through gates, determine correlation values, remove luck from the equation, and produce picks for every upcoming slate up to 3 days in advance.

Current State

  • Repository initialized with coordination board and site structure.
  • 8 unvalidated strategy templates defined (none validated) (STRAT-001 through STRAT-008).
  • 3-day slate pick framework defined; 31 official games listed in verified/schedule.html (NBA game IDs); 0 picks issued.
  • Injury tracking integrated with official NBA injury report (11/13/2026).
  • Game flow framework based on Pace and Dean Oliver's Four Factors.
  • No live odds feed, no line-generation model, no backtesting engine connected yet.

Key Findings

  • Minutes are the single most important variable for player props. A player's statistical output is almost perfectly correlated with minutes played. Predicting minutes more accurately than the bookmaker is the primary edge.
  • Correlation matters in parlays. Positively correlated legs (star points + team win + game over) hit together more often than independent odds suggest. Negatively correlated legs (two players from same team both Over) compete for the same possessions and should be avoided.
  • Injuries drive rotation minutes. An Out starter removes ~100% of their projected minutes and production, redistributing to backups and remaining starters. This directly affects player-prop lines.
  • Four Factors predict game outcomes. Dean Oliver's Four Factors (eFG% 40%, TOV% 25%, OREB% 20%, FTR 15%) correlate with team winning. The NBA FAQ states team PIE R² of .908 vs win %, as NBA’s own claim (NBA FAQ ↗). The .948 figure is not reproduced here.
  • Back-to-back road fatigue reduces pace. Estimated ~1.5 possession reduction for the fatigued team (unverified).

Strategic Recommendations

  • Connect live odds feeds (DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars) for originated line comparison.
  • Build the line-generation model using team-level Four Factors, pace, and injury-adjusted minutes projections.
  • Backtest all 8 strategy templates against 2024-25 and 2025-26 NBA seasons before any live wagering.
  • Implement the injury gate: any Out/Doubtful/Questionable starter triggers rotation-minutes review before pick approval.
  • Limit parlays to max 4 legs; max 1 prop per team per game; require documented correlation thesis for every parlay.
  • Deploy the GitHub Pages site with all data tables and flagged items for review.
