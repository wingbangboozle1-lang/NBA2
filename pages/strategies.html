Validation gates — specification

A strategy must clear all ten to be promoted and to receive rewards. Nine of the ten are currently blocked by missing data, which is the real status of this project and the reason the leaderboard is empty.

Validation gates — specification
Gate Name Rule Threshold Why it exists Basis Status Blocking reason SourceFlags
G-01 Sample size >= 300 settled bets for team markets; >= 500 for player props. 300 settled bets At -110 a 55% bettor needs roughly 300 bets before the standard error (sqrt(p(1-p)/n) ~ 2.9%) stops swamping a 5-point edge. Below this the leaderboard is ranking noise. derived from the binomial standard error at p=0.55 — see research/05-skill-vs-luck.md blocked No settled bets yet. Ledger opens with the 2026-11-13 slate. derivedDerived: nba2.models.lines FLAG-GATE-01
G-02 Closing line value (CLV) Mean CLV vs. closing number > 0 with a bootstrap 95% CI excluding 0. — flagged CLV is the single least-luck-dependent measure of whether a line beats the market. BLOCKED: requires a licensed odds feed (requirements R-101) blocked No market prices captured. Our lines are published, but nothing to beat them yet. derivedDerived: nba2.models.lines FLAG-GATE-01
G-03 Probability calibration Brier score <= 0.245 on settled games AND calibration slope in [0.85, 1.15] AND intercept in [-0.10, 0.10]. 0.245 brier 0.25 is the Brier score of always forecasting 50%; a line-originator must beat that and be calibrated, otherwise the stake sizing is fiction. derived: Brier (1950) definition; threshold is our own proposal (unratified) blocked Needs >= 100 settled games. derivedDerived: nba2.models.lines FLAG-GATE-01
G-04 Line accuracy vs. naive baselines Mean absolute error of our spread must beat BOTH baselines: (a) 0.0 spread, (b) raw point-differential-per-game, by >= 0.30 points. 0.3 points MAE A line that only reproduces the standings' scoring margin adds no work; if shrinkage and schedule adjustment do not reduce error, delete the model. derived from the snapshot baselines in nba2.models.gates blocked Needs settled games with official final scores. derivedDerived: nba2.models.lines FLAG-GATE-01
G-05 Luck removal Bootstrap (10,000 resamples, fixed seed) 95% CI of ROI excludes 0; t-stat >= 2.0; and the luck-adjusted PnL (each bet replaced by its model-expected PnL, variance kept) must not reverse the sign. 2.0 t-stat Separates 'won' from 'right'. A strategy whose PnL is explained by a few lucky bad beats fails here even if the record is positive. derived: standard bootstrap / t-test; see research/05-skill-vs-luck.md blocked Needs settled bets. derivedDerived: nba2.models.lines FLAG-GATE-01
G-06 Multiple-testing control Across every strategy ever evaluated in the same window, apply Holm-Bonferroni to the CLV t-tests and require the Deflated Sharpe Ratio > 0. 0.05 family-wise alpha A leaderboard with N trials will produce a 'best' strategy by pure search. Punishing the number of tries is the only defence. derived: Holm (1979); Deflated Sharpe = Bailey & Lopez de Prado (2014) formulation blocked Requires G-02 inputs and a complete trial ledger (see S-11). derivedDerived: nba2.models.lines FLAG-GATE-01
G-07 Walk-forward, no lookahead Every feature timestamped at or before tip-off; train on the first 70% of the window, test on the final 30%; test MAE must not exceed full-window MAE by more than 25%. 0.25 relative degradation Ratings built from a standings snapshot include future games for the teams that played earlier; that leak is silent and fatal. derived from our own ingest design (see research/01-line-origination.md 'point-in-time') at risk The current snapshot is a single as-of standings table, which is NOT point-in-time for games inside the week it covers. Backtests from this table would leak; only the forward ledger is clean. derivedDerived: nba2.models.lines FLAG-GATE-01
G-08 Parameter robustness Re-price the slate over the full sensitivity box of every unratified parameter; a pick is only published if its side is unchanged in >= 90% of the grid. 0.9 fraction of grid If a 10% change in sigma flips the pick, the pick is a parameter, not a signal. derived: implemented in nba2.models.gates.sensitivity_stability evaluated — derivedDerived: nba2.models.lines FLAG-GATE-01
G-09 Cost and capacity Survive -110 vig, 1% hard-cap stake, 25% Kelly, and a re-quote of the model at the reference price minus 0.25 points of expected slippage. 0.01 fraction of bankroll Lines that only win before the vig, or before you move size, are not tradeable. derived: REFERENCE_PRICE + staking config evaluated — derivedDerived: nba2.models.lines FLAG-GATE-01
G-10 Availability-adjustment guardrail If a player on the official report with an Out/Doubtful status cannot be mapped to a quantified minutes delta, the line is published availability-blind and the game is marked INJURY-BLIND. No bet may be Tier A on an injury-blind game after the 13:00 ET report on game day. — flagged Otherwise the model silently assumes injuries do not matter, which is exactly when a naive line is most wrong. official report structure: https://ak-static.cms.nba.com/referee/injury/ (no minutes fields) evaluated — derivedDerived: nba2.models.lines FLAG-GATE-01
