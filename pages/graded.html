The first card in this repository scored against official results. Nine games from 2026-11-13, priced from the ratings snapshot that predates every tip, graded against the final scores on NBA.com. The headline is deliberately unflattering: the totals beat a no-information baseline by 3.3 points per game, the margins beat it by 0.4, and with nine games neither result is statistically significant — about 1,565 graded games are needed to settle the question.
