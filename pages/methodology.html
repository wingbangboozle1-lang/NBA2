Read this first: no market price is available to this build, so nothing here is a “value bet” — lines are projections and picks are triggers. Team point rates come from a third-party table (win-loss is cross-checked against NBA.com). Blank means no source, never zero. See every open flag.

Model parameters — derived, not assumed

These are the only two structural numbers the whole site depends on. Both are fitted against the verified standings snapshot; where the fit is weak we say so instead of quietly picking a convention.
