Cross-game parlays, and what stacking costs

Two different games: the product rule is nearly right, so cross-game parlays are the safe place to express multiple views. Same game: the product rule is off by 13–26% on the ten quoted games above, which is larger than the entire edge a good model earns. And every leg you add multiplies the book's hold — arithmetic that needs no data to check.
