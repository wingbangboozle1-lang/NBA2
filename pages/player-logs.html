Player game logs (official, captured per player)

The brief asks for per-player game logs — minutes, points, rebounds, assists and the opponent — for at least the players who appear on an injury report. Until this session the repository had one game (gid 0022600231) and no per-player season baselines at all. This page publishes 5 players and 48 official player-games, every row read from the player's own nba.com game-log page on 2026-11-15.

Capture rule. every player listed Out / Questionable / Doubtful on the official injury release Injury-Report_2026-11-15_06_15PM.pdf whose club plays on 2026-11-16, excluding rows whose official reason begins 'G League' (a two-way or on-assignment player has no rotation minutes to redistribute). Two clubs of that slate, GSW and NOP, were captured in full in this session; the remaining clubs are listed under not_captured and are blank by policy, never estimated.
