Why each pick is withheld

The twelve gates a pick must pass
Gate Name Rule Basis Source Why it exists
G1 Source class Every input field resolves to an official NBA page or the official injury PDF. PROPOSED — policy nba.com/standings A third-party or unreachable source may not silently fill a competitive input.
G2 Completeness No required input is blank. A blank anywhere fails the strategy for that slate. PROPOSED — policy nba.com/stats/teams/traditional Blank ≠ zero. The prior build's rule, kept.
G3 Sample floor ≥ 10 official games observed by BOTH teams before a line may be graded above C. PROPOSED — derived from reliability nba.com/standings Below ~10 games the shrink weight λ drops under ~0.75 and the line is mostly league mean.
G4 Estimator agreement Two independent ratings (net points/game; NetRtg) must imply spreads within 2.0 pts. COMPUTED this build (observed max 1.0 pts) nba.com/stats/teams/advanced Two estimators that disagree mean the model is unstable, not that there is an edge.
G5 Total-estimator agreement PF/PA total and per-100×pace total must be within 4.0 pts. COMPUTED this build (observed max 4.25 pts) nba.com/stats/teams/traditional Large gaps flag pace/defence inconsistencies that would poison a totals bet.
G6 Market price present A dated, sourced price for the exact line must exist before a pick is staked. PROPOSED — policy nba.com/games Without a price there is no edge, no stake size and no ROI. Currently FAILS for every line.
G7 Backtest with settlement ≥ 300 settled bets of the strategy's own signals, settled from official box scores. PROPOSED — sample-size policy basketball-reference.com/teams/LAL/2027_gamelog.html 300 bets is the point at which a 55% hit rate separates from 52.4% at 5% one-sided.
G8 Out-of-sample holdout Signals fitted on the first 70% of the backtest window must hold on the last 30%. PROPOSED — policy basketball-reference.com/teams/LAL/2027_gamelog.html Prevents publishing a curve fit.
G9 Luck screen Every team the strategy relies on must be inside the luck noise band (|z| ≤ 1.96). COMPUTED this build — 30/30 pass nba.com/standings A signal leaning on a lucky record is measuring luck.
G10 Correlation honesty No parlay priced by multiplying legs unless the leg correlation is measured or bounded. PROPOSED — policy nba.com/schedule Same-game legs are not independent; the error is one-sided and expensive.
G11 Freshness Inputs must be dated within 24 h of the slate, and the injury report within its filing window. OFFICIAL (NBA filing deadlines) official.nba.com/nba-injury-report-2026-27-season/ Teams must file by 5 p.m. local the day before, and 11 a.m.–1 p.m. on game day.
G12 No look-ahead No input timestamped after the decision time may enter that decision. PROPOSED — policy basketball-reference.com/teams/LAL/2027_gamelog.html The single most common way a profitable backtest is fake.
