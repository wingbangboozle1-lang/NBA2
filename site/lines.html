Our own number for every game in the window, with the two independent estimators that make it, their disagreement, the fitted parameters behind them, and a source for every input.

Formula, in full.
rating = mean(shrunk net points per game, shrunk NetRtg converted to points per game)
margin = rating_home + h − rating_away
p(home win) = Φ(margin / σ)   spread = −round_to_half(margin)
total = (PF_home+PA_away)/2 + Δ_home/2 + (PF_away+PA_home)/2 + Δ_away/2 where Δ is the team's own scoring margin. Every parameter is fitted (see the table below); nothing is hand-set.

Spread A uses the net-points rating; spread B uses NetRtg. They are computed from different official numerators (points vs points-per-100-possessions), so agreement is evidence the line is stable. Δ total compares the per-game-average total with a per-100-possession × pace total; a large gap flags a pace inconsistency and drops the game to grade C.

The honest problem with σ and h. Fitting both freely against one season of win totals is ill-conditioned: σ anywhere from 9 to 13 fits equally well with h from +7.9 down to 0.0. This build therefore pins h to the home-win rate actually observed (89–84 over 173 home games) and fits σ alone. The unconstrained answer is printed beside it rather than hidden.
