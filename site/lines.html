Originated lines

Our own number for every game in the window, with the two independent estimators that make it, their disagreement, the fitted parameters behind them, and a source for every input.

Formula, in full.
rating = mean(shrunk net points per game, shrunk NetRtg converted to points per game)
margin = rating_home + h − rating_away
p(home win) = Φ(margin / σ)   spread = −round_to_half(margin)
Total = possessions × points-per-possession — poss = (Pace_h + Pace_a)/2, PPP_h = (OffRtg_h/100 + DefRtg_a/100)/2, total = poss · (PPP_h + PPP_a). This is deliberately not an average of the two teams' scoring averages (that average is published beside it as "Total B" for comparison, and a large gap between them flags a pace inconsistency). Every parameter is fitted or read from the official tables (see the table below); nothing is hand-set.

Market-anchored prior (framework live, value blank). When a licensed closing line exists (gate G6) each line publishes a blend of our line and the closing line weighted by out-of-sample log-loss: w_i = (1/LL_i) / Σ(1/LL_j) — the better-calibrated forecaster gets more weight. With no odds feed in this repository the column stays blank and flagged (FLAG-V2-NO-MARKET-PRICE); the rule is frozen in nba2/v2/model.py::market_prior and its pre-registration hash is on the Official results ledger.

Spread A uses the net-points rating; spread B uses NetRtg. They are computed from different official numerators (points vs points-per-100-possessions), so agreement is evidence the line is stable. The headline total is possessions × PPP; "Total B" is the scoring-average estimator. Δ total compares them; a large gap flags a pace inconsistency and drops the game to grade C.

The honest problem with σ and h. Fitting both freely against one season of win totals is ill-conditioned: σ anywhere from 9 to 13 fits equally well with h from +7.9 down to 0.0. This build therefore pins h to the home-win rate actually observed (89–84 over 173 home games) and fits σ alone. The unconstrained answer is printed beside it rather than hidden.
