The honest reading. The model beats a coin flip on log-loss and matches the better-record rule, but the paired test — the only test that asks whether the model adds information the record does not already carry — comes back at p = 0.711. That is not evidence of skill. It is the reason every stake on this site is 0.00, and it is published at the top of the brief rather than in a footnote.

Luck, as a z-score per club

Expected wins come from the club's own Pythagorean rate over its official games; the residual is divided by the binomial sd of that club's own game count. A club outside |z| > 2 is a club whose record is mostly outcome noise, and a strategy that leans on one is refused, not adjusted.

The transcribed page, checked against the results

The 2026-11-19 advanced page was transcribed by hand. Two independent routes to the same ranking are compared here — a margin rating solved from the committed finals, and the league's own per-100 NetRtg — and the Pearson correlation between them is r = 0.979 over 30 clubs. A transcription error in one club's row would drop that number; three arithmetic identities in test_v7 check the rest.
