The site was publishing a pre-final snapshot as if it were current. Reconstructed against today's official standings, the 30 team records on this site agree with the pre-11/13-finals state on 30 of 30 rows and with current official standings on 12 of 30. Nine games were played after the table was transcribed. Nothing on the site could have caught it, because nothing compared a published row against a fresher capture. Every stale row is listed here.
The first genuine forecast was graded, and it is not yet evidence of skill. nba2/data/picks.json was committed 83 minutes before the earliest tip. It went 4-0 on the 4 distinct games it bet — but 8 wins are recorded, because two strategies fired on the same four games. On all 9 graded games the model named the winner 5/9, the same as a naive net-rating baseline and worse than always picking the home team (7/9). No profit is published, because no signal in the file carries a price.
What did not change. Zero picks are stakable, because no market price exists. The two sealed ledgers still start on 2026-11-14 and correctly remain PENDING. Nothing was invented to fill a gap, and the two irregularities found (a sign inversion in the spread selections, and two dashboards claiming to be the site) are published on the Flagged page rather than fixed silently in someone else's file.

1. Six things you need to know, in the order they affect a decision

What was built. A v2 line-origination model over a 31-game, three-day window (2026-11-13 → 2026-11-16), originated only from official NBA inputs, with two independent ratings estimators, a fitted market-agnostic total model, published formulas, and a per-row source link on every table.
What is verified. 31/31 games match the official schedule; all 30 team records match live nba.com/standings exactly; 85 injury rows match the official 11/13 04:15 PM ET PDF row for row; team scoring, opponent scoring and advanced ratings are official nba.com/stats tables.
What is not possible yet. No price exists, so no pick can be staked. Official per-game finals now exist for 155 of 174 games (captured from nba.com by a parallel session, with 19 games unreachable), so the method CAN be backtested — and it has been. All 31 picks remain withheld because a pick needs a price, not because the method is unknown.
The skill finding. A walk-forward test on 139 official finals puts this rating method at 66.9% straight-up (95% CI 58.7-74.2%), margin MAE 11.91 against 13.27 for a zero baseline, Brier 0.2184 against 0.2500 for a coin flip. That is real predictive signal - the first measured in this repository. It is accuracy, NOT profit: there is still no price to beat, so no ROI exists.
The compression finding. The same test shows predicted margins are compressed to 30% of true dispersion (sd 4.98 vs 16.43) and correlate with the real margin at only +0.39. The model ranks games; it does not forecast blowouts, and no stake may ever be sized from a predicted margin.
The luck finding. Across 30 teams, zero are outside the |z| ≤ 1.96 luck band. No team in this window can honestly be called over- or under-performing its underlying scoring level, so no strategy may lean on that story.
The correlation finding. Measured on 30 teams: net points vs NetRtg r = +0.999; either vs win% r ≈ +0.93. Measured on 155 finals: spread vs total r = +0.018 (95% CI -0.15 to +0.18), i.e. indistinguishable from zero. Still unmeasurable: every player prop correlation, because no player box scores are reachable.
The recommended path. Licensed odds remain the only hard blocker for anything involving money. Scheduled ingestion keeps the snapshot fresh; per-game results are now partially available (19 games still missing). With odds plus 300 settled bets the leaderboard becomes computable; until then the honest product is a projection desk that grades its own lines.

Recommended sequence

  1. Connect the odds feed (SG-3). Nothing that involves money moves without a price. All other model work is preparation until then.
  2. Connect a per-game results feed (SG-2). This turns the model into something testable and lets the leaderboard exist.
  3. Schedule the ingestion (SG-1). Freshness gate G11 can never pass while data arrives by hand.
  4. Then measure the six missing correlations and re-price parlays; then pre-register strategies and start the competition.

Everything else on this site — gates, leaderboard rules, injury tracking, game flow — is designed to be ready when those three feeds land.
