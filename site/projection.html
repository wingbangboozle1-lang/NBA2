Projections

This is the projection surface: what the model believes about each of the 34 games in the window 2026-11-15 → 2026-11-18. It carries no price, no trigger, no lean and no stake, because it does not need one — a projection is a statement about a game, and it is safe to publish whether or not anyone can bet it.

Safe to publish. Nothing on this page is a bet recommendation. The companion bet surface is gated and currently withholds every stake; the two are never mixed on one page, so a reader can always tell a projection from a wager.
The headline is the disagreement, not the blend. Two independent estimators are computed for every game — one from official points-per-game differential, one from official NetRtg — and the number to read first is how far apart they land. The blended spread sits underneath it, and a game whose estimators disagree by more than 2.0 points is graded down for exactly that reason.
The total is possessions × points-per-possession. Possessions come from the two clubs' own official paces interacting against the league pace, and points-per-possession from each side's official offensive rating against the opponent's official defensive rating. An average of scoring averages ignores the pace of the actual matchup; it is still computed, and the gap between the two is printed on the originated lines page.

What the rotation column can and cannot say

Rotation availability is the official injury report (designations per club) joined to measured minutes shares. Both halves are only as good as the capture: 2 of the 84 players on the latest official report appear in a captured box score, because 2 of 188 official finals have had their per-player logs read line by line. Where a club has no captured rotation the minutes column is blank with that reason rather than estimated. See Injuries & rotation for the per-club table.
