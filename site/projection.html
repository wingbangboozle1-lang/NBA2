This page is the projection surface. It contains no stake, no edge and no market price, and that is a rule enforced in code, not a style choice. A projected margin and a projected total are descriptions of a game we can price from official inputs. They are not bets. The trigger price in the tables is the boundary at which acting would stop being irrational at fair odds; it is not a recommendation, and it is not compared with any real price because no odds feed is connected (gate G6).

What the two estimators say, and how far apart they are

The headline number on every row is the disagreement between the two independent estimators, not their average. The average is published underneath it, because averaging two methods that disagree is how a model hides the fact that it does not know.

The band is |z| ≤ 2.0. Two sensitivity columns re-compute the same z at exponents 13.5 and 16.5 so a reader can see the band membership is not an artefact of the exponent choice.

A strategy that leans on a club outside the band is refused, not downgraded.
