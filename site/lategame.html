What this is. The brief asks for game-flow analysis. True pace / garbage-time decomposition needs play-by-play (blocked, R-047), but the part that is measurable from the official quarter table is published here. Inflation is how much more (or less) the two teams combined scored in Q4 than in the average of Q1–Q3. Positive inflation is the signature of late-game (garbage-time) scoring; a large negative z is a late-game tightening. Adj total is the game total with the Q4 inflation removed (early total + early rate) — a derived control, never a forecast. Every number is re-derived from the committed official quarter scores by nba2/v3/gameflow.py.
