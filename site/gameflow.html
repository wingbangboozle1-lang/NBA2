Three flow effects that are real, and what each needs

Rest and density measurable now

Rest days and games-in-window come straight from the official schedule and are printed above. The effect of a back-to-back on scoring and pace still needs settled results to quantify.

Garbage time needs play-by-play

Late-game minutes in a decided game suppress stars' counting stats and inflate bench usage. Requires per-possession data with score margin and clock.

Foul trouble needs play-by-play

A star's minutes are truncated by two early fouls in a way no season average shows. Requires foul timestamps.

Why flow analysis is not in the line yet

Including a variable you cannot measure is worse than omitting it: it makes the line look more informed than it is. The line on the previous page uses only inputs whose official source is on that page. Flow effects are listed here as known-missing so they can be added with evidence, not assumed away.
