What 'verified' means here — and what it does not

  • Verified means: the value on the page equals the value on the official page on the retrieval date, and the link to that page is on the row.
  • Verified does not mean correct today. Every dataset on this site is a snapshot. Scores, records and statuses change hourly; the snapshot date is printed on every page.
  • Derived values are not source-verified. A fitted σ, a shrunk rating or a line is computed by us from verified inputs. Its formula and parameters are published so it can be re-derived, and it is badged "derived" rather than "official".
  • Nothing is filled in. If a value had no source, the cell is blank and carries the name of the missing input.
