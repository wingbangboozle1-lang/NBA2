Validation gates

The gates are the only thing standing between a research number and something a reader might act on. They are listed here with the basis for each threshold, because a gate whose number comes from nowhere is a gate that can be argued away.

The gate that matters today is G6

There is no market price in this repository for any game. Without a price there is no edge, no stake and no return, so every strategy that depends on comparing our number with a price is blocked — not conservatively, but definitionally.

Evaluation of the gate set as built

  • Good: G1–G5 and G9 are computed from official inputs on every build, so they cannot silently pass. G12 (no look-ahead) is audited against the data with zero violations recorded.
  • Weak: G7 (≥300 settled bets) and G8 (70/30 holdout) are sample-size policies with no published power calculation, so their numbers cannot be defended if challenged.
  • Missing: no gate tests whether a strategy's result survives the number of strategies tried. Selecting the best of nine preregistered strategies and reporting its hit rate without a multiple-comparison correction is the single most common way a leaderboard manufactures skill.
  • Self-referential: the site's own freshness gate (CC-FRESH-01) fails in the repository's checker and is excluded from blocking by the publish workflow — so the one check that says "this is stale" is the one check allowed to fail.
