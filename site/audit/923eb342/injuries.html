The archive gap

The repository's own injury archive holds three releases with a newest stamp of 11/17/26 11:00 AM (index read 2026-11-17T16:41Z). The official index lists 2026-11-19 releases from 12:00 AM ET to at least 07:00 AM ET. So no archived release exists for 11/18 or 11/19, and no consecutive-report diff can exist for them — the diff feature is intact but starved (F-923-04).

Filing rules that the retrieval schedule has to match

From the official injury-report page: teams must designate a participation status by 5 p.m. local time the day before a game, and for the second game of a back-to-back by 1 p.m. local time on game day; a game-day report is due between 11 a.m. and 1 p.m. local (8–10 a.m. for tip-offs at 5 p.m. or earlier); and reports are updated on a continual basis through the day. Source: official.nba.com/nba-injury-report-2026-27-season/. That last clause is why the index — not a fixed daily time — is the thing to poll.

Injury effect on rotation minutes

Cannot be produced from what is held. Rotation and minutes redistribution need per-player logs for the players involved, and coverage of injury-report players is 12 of 116 (10.3%): github.com/wingbangboozle1-lang/NBA2/blob/main/data/v6/…. This page therefore prints the official status and stops there, rather than estimating an effect.
