Sources & method

What was read, when, with what transport, and what could not be read at all. The method is part of the evidence: a number read with a fragile transport at an unrecorded time is worth less than one read with a timestamp.

Transport, stated plainly

Direct TLS from the execution sandbox to www.nba.com, official.nba.com, ak-static.cms.nba.com and stats.nba.com fails with SSL_ERROR_SYSCALL (HTTP 000). Every official read in this session therefore used the platform's page-fetch transport, which returns the page as text. Each fetch is timestamped (2026-11-19T12:20Z) and recorded below. This is a real read of the real page; it is not a scrape of a cached copy.
