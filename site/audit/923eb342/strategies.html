Parlays and prop combos

A parlay's price is the measured joint probability, and the naive product is printed beside it rather than withheld. Same-game legs are not independent: the site already demonstrates a ±26% price error from treating them as independent, so the joint pricing is the whole point of the page. Player-prop combos are the product the brief asks for most directly and the one most dependent on data that is not held: same-player correlation needs per-player box scores, and coverage of injury-report players is 10.3%. Both remain blocked, and blocking them is the correct outcome until the logs exist.

Leaderboard

The leaderboard pays nobody while a gate fails, which is the right design. It has also never paid anybody, because G6 has never passed. A leaderboard in that state is a list of unproven claims, and it should be labelled that way wherever it appears — see SG-923-10 for the minimum disclosure that would make it honest.
