Observed Sharpe / DSR— no settled PnLblank by policy, see note
Why this exists. A leaderboard that tries N strategies and rewards the best produces a winner by search, not by skill. The defence is the Deflated Sharpe Ratio (Bailey & López de Prado, 2014): the probability the observed Sharpe beats the best-of-N-trials Sharpe expected under no skill. It needs three inputs — the observed Sharpe, the observation count, and the trial count — and the trial count was the missing one (G-06 sat at BLOCKED "no trial ledger"). This registry makes it real. The break-even Sharpe is the bar G-06 imposes: with 10 trials over 139 observations a strategy must show a Sharpe above 0.2788 to pass.
What is blank. The observed Sharpe is a function of settled PnL. There is no market price (gate G6) and no settled bet, so no return exists. The bar is published; the observed SR and the resulting DSR per strategy are left blank and flagged, never invented.

Definition

DSR = Phi((SR - E[max SR under no skill]) * sqrt(T-1) / sqrt(1 - g3*SR + (g4-1)/4 * SR^2)); E[max SR] grows with the trial count, so more tries raise the bar. G-06 passes when DSR > 0.95.
