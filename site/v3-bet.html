How settlement will work when a price exists

A pick settles only from the official final score after its game page's quarter scores have been read and confirmed to sum to the printed final. The settlement stamp stays visible on the row. A pick whose game has not finished stays open, and an open pick is never counted as a win in any rate on this site.

Rotation availability — its own column

The brief asks for rotation availability as a column on the picks page, not a footnote. It is here: each pick carries the availability of both clubs at the time of sealing, taken from the official injury report revision named on the injuries page.

The model does not consume it. A club whose top-eight minutes are redistributed is priced as its season average, and FLAG-V3-ROTATION-UNFILED records that the minutes model has not been filed for it. The column is disclosure, not an input.
