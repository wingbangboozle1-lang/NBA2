Parlays: the measured rho, printed next to the naive product

Two legs on one game are not independent. The price of the pair is P(A) × P(B | A); a correlation-blind tool prints P(A) × P(B). Both are below, with the difference in a badge. The measured correlation between home margin and total points over 210 official finals is r = -0.0440 (95% CI -0.178 … 0.092) — statistically indistinguishable from zero, which is itself the useful finding: stacking a spread and a total on the same game costs about what independence says it costs, and any book pricing a same-game parlay materially worse than the naive product is pricing something we cannot see.
