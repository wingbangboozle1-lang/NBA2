Notes on method for a reviewer

  • Pages were read on 2026-11-14 through the agent's page-fetch tool; direct TLS from the sandbox to nba.com fails, which is recorded as F-004's neighbour on the verification page.
  • Tables were transcribed into code (nba2/v2/official.py) with one named field per column, so a transcription error is a visible diff rather than a hidden one.
  • Arithmetic identities (points scored = points allowed, home wins = road losses) are asserted on every build; they caught nothing this time, which is the point of running them.
