Player props & combos

What the official player table gives us, and the one thing it does not: a distribution.

No prop is publishable. Season averages exist officially; the probability that a player clears a line needs a per-game distribution and a minutes floor. Neither is available, so the site publishes the averages and the missing input by name rather than a fabricated probability.

Season averages are a mean, not a distribution. To price "over 24.5 points" you need the spread of game-by-game scoring around that mean and the chance the player is on the floor at all — which is why the minutes-floor column is blank rather than guessed.

Prop combos: the same correlation problem, per player

A points + rebounds combo needs the correlation between a player's own points and rebounds in the same game. For a centre it is usually strongly positive; for a guard it can be near zero. Publishing a combo price without it would be a guess dressed as a number, so combos are blocked here (S6) with the exact dataset named.

What would unblock this page

  • Per-player game logs (one row per player per game: minutes, points, rebounds, assists) → distributions and combo correlations.
  • A licensed odds feed with player prop lines and prices → the line to beat.
  • An empirical status→play table (from report history) → the probability a "Questionable" player appears at all.
