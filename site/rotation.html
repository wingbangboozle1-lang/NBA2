The redistribution arithmetic

A club plays 240 player-minutes in a regulation game. With the top 8 by minutes per game, share_i = minutes_i / 240. When player k is out, incumbents absorb 65% of the freed minutes in proportion to their existing shares and the next man absorbs 35%. The model is in nba2/v3/rotation.py and is exercised by the test suite on a synthetic roster; it produces no number for a real club until the minutes capture lands.
