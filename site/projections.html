Totals as possessions x points-per-possession

Expected possessions are the mean of the two clubs' official PACE columns. Each side's points per possession is the mean of its own OffRtg and the opponent's DefRtg, divided by 100. The total is then possessions x (ppp_home + ppp_away) — not the average of two scoring averages, which would ignore what each defence does.

Why the two surfaces are separate

A projection says what the model believes. A bet says what to risk. Mixing them is how a projection acquires authority it has not earned. The projection surface above is complete and published; the bet surface below it is deliberately empty.

Market-anchored prior

The brief asks for a prior that blends the model line with the closing line, weighted by measured out-of-sample log-loss. The weighting rule is w_market = (1/LL_mkt) / (1/LL_mkt + 1/LL_model); blended = w_model*model + w_market*close. The model's own log-loss is measured: it is the point-in-time walk-forward log-loss from the committed backtest over official finals. The closing-line log-loss is blank because no licensed odds feed is connected (gate G6), so the market weight is 0 and the blended line equals the model line until a feed lands. The measured weight and the rule are published so the blend is a one-line recompute once a closing line exists — nothing is invented.
