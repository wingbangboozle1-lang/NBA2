Settlement policy

Verified finals only. A game is settled when its final score appears on the official game page; a game without one remains UNSETTLED indefinitely rather than being filled from memory, a box score that disagrees, or any third party. Quarter arrays must sum to the finals — they do on every row above, and the build fails closed if they ever do not.

Tamper-evident projection ledger (per-pick hashes)

Every published projection row is hashed (sha256 of the canonical row JSON) and the hash is committed to git before tip-off — that commit is the pre-tip publication. At settlement the row is re-hashed and compared. Any edit after publication shows up as a hash mismatch.
