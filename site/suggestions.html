If you only do three things

  1. Connect a licensed odds feed (SG-3). Everything about edge, stake and ROI depends on it. Today the site correctly publishes zero picks because it has no price — that is a data gap, not a modelling gap.
  2. Get per-game results (SG-2). It turns every projection into something scoreable and lets the leaderboard exist.
  3. Schedule the ingestion (SG-1). Hand-made snapshots cannot satisfy a freshness gate, so no strategy can ever be ranked on fresher-than-today data.

Suggestions about this site's own design

Split projection from bet

Done on this site: the lines page publishes projections, the picks page publishes nothing because gate G6 fails. Mixing them is how a research desk becomes an unreliable tipster.

Put the source on every row

Done: the builder refuses to emit a row without a URL, so the compliance is structural rather than a promise.

Make uncertainty visible

Done: σ, the reliability weight, the two estimators and their disagreement are printed per game instead of a single confident-looking number.

Give every blank a reason

Done: an empty cell is always accompanied by the missing input's name, so absence is information rather than an oversight.

What I would not do

  • Do not add a 'confidence score' built from conviction alone. It is |margin|/σ, which grows for lopsided games, not for well-understood ones. The estimator-disagreement column is the honest analogue.
  • Do not publish props before minutes data exists. A season average is not a distribution, and a prop mispriced by a distributional assumption is indistinguishable from a bad pick.
  • Do not rank strategies on win rate. Twelve bets can look skilful and be noise; log-loss on 300 settled bets is the cheapest defence.
