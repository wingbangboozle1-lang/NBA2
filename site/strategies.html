Strategies & Validation Gates

Signal-generation strategies, the validation gates each must pass, and the leaderboard.

Strategy definitions

Five strategies covering single bets, parlays, and player-prop combos.

StrategyTypeDescription
Margin Favorite (Single)singleSingle straight bet on the model's outright favorite where model win probability clears the edge threshold.
Spread Value (Single)singleSingle bet on the favorite to cover when the model's predicted margin is strong (clears the strong-margin threshold).
Totals (Single)singleSingle over/under on the originated total where the predicted-total edge clears the threshold.
Lean Parlay (Combo)parlay2–3 single-bet legs from different games, each clearing the edge gate, combined into a parlay (correlation gate: distinct games).
Player Prop CombopropPlayer-prop combination (over/under player totals). Requires verified player-level data; values left blank until sourced.

Validation gates

Every strategy must pass these gates before its signal is eligible.

GateRequirement
G1_data_integrityAll factual inputs trace to an official verified source; model outputs are labeled Model.
G2_edgeModel edge clears break-even (0.524) / thresholds.
G3_correlationMulti-leg parlays use legs from distinct games (low correlation); same-game legs excluded.
G4_sample_sizeMinimum observations before a strategy is trusted (provisional otherwise).
G5_variance_luckSkill-based selection; realized results checked for luck vs skill (see correlations).

Removing luck

Luck is removed by requiring: a documented skill basis, a minimum sample size, and realized results statistically distinguishable from chance (binomial test vs .500, ROI convergence). New strategies are provisional until tracked.
