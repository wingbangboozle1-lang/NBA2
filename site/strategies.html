Why a strategy cannot simply be declared 'profitable'

A line that agrees with the market is not an edge; a line that disagrees is either an edge or an error, and only a settled backtest can tell you which. Both parlays types add a second problem: the legs are correlated, and multiplying them is wrong in a direction that costs money.

Leaderboard rules and reward proposal

Leaderboard competition design — needs owner sign-off
Rule Proposal Why it matters Status Basis
Scoring metric Log-loss on settled bets, not win rate Win rate rewards luck on small samples; log-loss is a proper scoring rule, so honest probabilities beat lucky ones. PROPOSED nba.com/standings
Minimum sample to rank 300 settled bets Below that the 95% interval on a 55% hit rate includes 50%. PROPOSED nba.com/games?date=2026-11-13
Trial accounting Every submitted strategy counts, including discarded ones Otherwise a submitter re-rolls variants until one looks good; the family-wise error rate explodes. PROPOSED nba.com/standings
Closure/verification A pick must be published before tip and be sealed with a hash Prevents back-filling a winning pick after the result is known. PROPOSED nba.com/schedule?date=2026-11-15
Unit accounting Stake in units of bankroll, capped at 1% per bet Keeps the leaderboard a skill measure rather than a leverage measure. PROPOSED nba.com/games
Reward split 100 units: 50 to the top log-loss score over a full season, 30 split across the next three, 20 held in escrow for out-of-sample validation Pays for demonstrated calibration, not a hot streak. PROPOSED — needs owner sign-off nba.com/games
Disqualifiers Any pick without a pre-tip timestamp, any settlement not from an official box score, any strategy edited after settlement begins Removes the three ways a leaderboard is usually gamed. PROPOSED nba.com/games?date=2026-11-13
Nothing here can run yet. All seven rules assume settled bets. With no price and no per-game results there are zero settled bets, so the leaderboard currently has nothing to score. The design is published so it can be approved or amended before the data arrives, not after.
