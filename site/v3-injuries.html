DIFFERENT SLATES: the two reports describe different game dates and share no (team, player) pair, so this diff measures slate turnover, not status change. It is published because a reader is entitled to see the arithmetic, and it is labelled because a percentage without this sentence would be a false claim.

A row is 'added' or 'dropped' only against the same (game date, team, player) key. A player who appears on a different game date is reported as a date move, not as a status change, because the two reports cover different slates.

Minutes redistribution — filed or not

The brief asks for top-eight minutes shares plus replacement shares when a player is out. The method is written (model.minutes_shares) and described on the methodology page.

The inputs are not in the repository: per-player game logs for at least the injury-report players are absent, so no club's rotation has been filed and no replacement share has been computed. Every affected line carries FLAG-V3-ROTATION-UNFILED. The page does not estimate redistribution from season averages, because a season average is precisely the quantity an absence changes.
