Teams & the two estimators

The headline confidence signal on this table is Δ estimators — how far apart the two independent ratings of the same team land. The consensus rating sits underneath it, deliberately not first: a small disagreement is what makes the consensus worth reading, and it is the honest analogue of a "confidence score" (which conviction alone cannot be — see the suggestions). Every row's inputs link to the official pages; PF/G and PA/G are rebuilt from the 205 official finals and cross-checked row-by-row against the three official team-stat pages.

Read the columns. Δ est = |Est A − Est B| in points. Est A shrinks net points per game; Est B shrinks NetRtg × Pace / 100 (points per game form). Consensus = their mean. λ is the measured reliability weight each estimator is shrunk by. Luck z = (wins − expected wins)/sd under the fitted league model; no strategy may lean on a team outside |z| ≤ 1.96 and none is outside it today.
