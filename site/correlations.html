Parlay correlation gate

Different-game outcomes are treated as approximately independent (near-zero correlation). Same-game legs are high-correlation and are excluded from parlays.

This gate (G3) keeps parlay legs independent so the system avoids self-correlated combos that inflate variance without adding skill.

Luck vs. skill

Luck is removed by requiring: a documented skill basis, a minimum sample size, and realized results statistically distinguishable from chance (binomial test vs .500, ROI convergence). New strategies are provisional until tracked.
