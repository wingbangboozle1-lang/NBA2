5. Why no profit number appears anywhere

no row in picks.json carries a market price (`american_odds` is absent from every signal), so profit is not measurable and is not published. A PnL here would be an assumed -110, i.e. an invented number.

A PnL would require assuming a price, and the assumption would be doing all the work: at −110 a 4-0 record is 3.64 units, at +150 it is 6.00. Publishing either would be publishing the assumption, not the model. The site therefore reports what was measured — wins, losses, error — and says plainly that the money question is unanswered until a price exists.

6. The verdict this page supports

the first genuine out-of-sample forecast the repository has: 8 of 8 graded signal rows won as published, across 4 distinct games. Those rows are double-counted by design - the moneyline and the spread fired on the same four games - and the spread half of them was published on the wrong side of the line (S-26): the same four rows read 4-0 as printed and 3-1 on the side the strategy names. The model called the winner in 5 of 9 games, a naive net-rating baseline also got 5 and always-home got more; no sample this small separates skill from luck, and no market price exists at all, so profit is not measurable.

Two conclusions follow, and they point in opposite directions, which is why both are printed:

  • Against the model: on nine games it named the winner no better than a naive net-rating baseline and worse than always picking the home team. No edge is demonstrated.
  • For the process: the gate machinery did its job — 4 of 4 S2 rows, and the same count for the moneyline rows — 8 rows in all rows graded WIN as published, and the site still refuses to rank the strategy, because four games is not a sample. A system that can report a clean sweep and simultaneously refuse to be impressed by it is the only kind worth building.
  • And the correction that came with re-checking it: the four spread rows won on the wrong side of the line — the v1 generator attached the underdog's number to the favourite's name (S-26). The generator is fixed and regression-tested; the historical file is left byte-identical, because its pre-tip provenance is worth more than a tidy restatement. Re-graded on the side the strategy names, the record is 3-1.
