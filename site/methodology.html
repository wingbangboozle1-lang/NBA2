Methodology

The whole model, in the order it runs, with every parameter and its identification.

1. Inputs

Team records and home/road splits from the official standings; points for and against per game from the official team and opponent tables; offensive, defensive and net rating, pace and PIE from the official advanced table; the slate from the official schedule; injuries from the official report PDF. All 10 URLs are on the sources page.

2. Ratings

Two independent estimators, each shrunk toward the league mean by the measured reliability weight λ = τ²/(τ² + σ²/games):

A: net points per game  r_A(t) = mean + λ · (PF(t) − PA(t) − mean)
B: net rating → points  r_B(t) = mean + λ · (NetRtg(t) · Pace(t)/100 − mean)
rating(t) = (r_A + r_B) / 2

τ² is estimated, not assumed: τ² = Var(net points) − mean(σ²/games). Observed λ is 0.778 on average, so a 10-game record is treated as about 22% noise.

3. Calibration

σ is fitted by least squares against the 30 official win totals, with h pinned to the measured home-win rate: h = σ(2p − 1) where p = 0.5294 over 187 home games. Result: σ = 13.06 pts, h = +0.77 pts, RMSE 1.054 wins versus 2.872 for a coin-flip baseline. Fitting both freely gives σ = 11.44, h = +6.00 — the same RMSE to three decimals, which is why the pair is not trusted.

4. Lines

margin   = rating(home) + h − rating(away)          # positive → home wins by
spread   = −round_to_nearest_half(margin)            # home handicap
p(home)  = Φ(margin / σ)
fair odds= 1/p  and  1/(1−p)                         # no hold applied
total    = possessions × PPP                        # NOT an average of scoring averages
poss     = (Pace(home) + Pace(away)) / 2
PPP(home)= (OffRtg(home)/100 + DefRtg(away)/100) / 2
total    = poss · (PPP(home) + PPP(away))

The headline total is possessions × points-per-possession. The scoring-average estimator Σ_team [ (PF(team)+PA(opp))/2 + Δ/2 ] is published beside it as "Total B"; the gap between the two is printed per game and any gap over 4.0 pts forces grade C.

4a. Market-anchored prior (framework, blank values)

blended = w_model · model_line + w_market · closing_line
w_i     = (1/LL_i) / Σ_j (1/LL_j)                    # LL = out-of-sample log-loss

The better-calibrated forecaster (smaller OOS log-loss) gets more weight. With no licensed odds feed (G6) the blend stays blank and flagged — the rule is frozen in nba2/v2/model.py::market_prior and pre-registered.

4b. Minutes redistribution (explicit)

top-8 share_i = mpg_i / Σ_top8 mpg
new_mpg_j     = mpg_j + Σ_{i out} mpg_i · mpg_j / Σ_{k available} mpg_k

The injury report decides who is out; this rule prices where the minutes go. Clubs without captured official per-club tables publish blank shares (never estimated). Triggers are published in both quote formats: decimal ↔ American, including the totals trigger (fair at our own total = +100 by construction; a market total T prices as 1−Φ((T−total)/σ_tot) with σ_tot measured from the official finals).

4c. Rest and travel

days_rest  = calendar days between consecutive games (0 = back-to-back)
3-in-4     = this game plus ≥2 more inside the 4-day window ending today
travel leg = haversine miles between the arena of the previous game and this one

Arena names/cities are official; coordinates are third-party (Wikimedia geocode, labelled) and distances are DERIVED values published beside their sources. A leg whose previous venue is unknown is blank and flagged.

5. Grading (not betting)

A = both spread estimators within 1.0 pt, |margin| ≥ 0.75σ, both total estimators within 2.0 pts. B = passes the minimum gates. C = fails one. A grade is a statement about model stability, never about expected profit. Expected profit requires a price, which is why every pick is withheld.

6. Luck

Expected wins come from the fitted model on each team's own point differential; the residual is luck, standardised by sqrt(games·p·(1−p)). Observed: 0 of 30 teams outside |z| ≤ 1.96. That means no team's record in this window is distinguishable from its underlying scoring level.

7. What would change the answer

  • A price (any price) turns a projection into a testable claim.
  • Per-game results turn a testable claim into a score.
  • Player logs turn injuries and props from qualitative notes into numbers.
  • More games narrow σ and h, which are currently identified loosely against a single season.

8. Reproduce it

python3 -m nba2.v2                # rebuilds data/v2/*.json and site/
python3 -m nba2.v2.check          # every row sourced, every blank explained
python3 -m nba2.v2.strategy_lab   # strategies, gates, correlations, luck separation
python3 -m unittest discover -s nba2/v2 -t . -p 'test_*.py'   # 167 tests, counted from the files
python3 -m unittest discover -s nba2/tests -t .                # 92 tests
python3 -m unittest discover -s backtest/tests                 # 49 tests

Those three figures are counted from the test files during this build, not copied from a commit message — a remembered count is the most reliably stale number on the site (F-033).
