Methodology

The whole model, in the order it runs, with every parameter and its identification.

1. Inputs

Team records and home/road splits from the official standings; points for and against per game from the official team and opponent tables; offensive, defensive and net rating, pace and PIE from the official advanced table; the slate from the official schedule; injuries from the official report PDF. All 10 URLs are on the sources page.

2. Ratings

Two independent estimators, each shrunk toward the league mean by the measured reliability weight λ = τ²/(τ² + σ²/games):

A: net points per game  r_A(t) = mean + λ · (PF(t) − PA(t) − mean)
B: net rating → points  r_B(t) = mean + λ · (NetRtg(t) · Pace(t)/100 − mean)
rating(t) = (r_A + r_B) / 2

τ² is estimated, not assumed: τ² = Var(net points) − mean(σ²/games). Observed λ is 0.788 on average, so a 10-game record is treated as about 21% noise.

3. Calibration

σ is fitted by least squares against the 30 official win totals, with h pinned to the measured home-win rate: h = σ(2p − 1) where p = 0.5275 over 182 home games. Result: σ = 12.95 pts, h = +0.71 pts, RMSE 1.045 wins versus 2.872 for a coin-flip baseline. Fitting both freely gives σ = 11.32, h = +6.00 — the same RMSE to three decimals, which is why the pair is not trusted.

4. Lines

margin   = rating(home) + h − rating(away)          # positive → home wins by
spread   = −round_to_nearest_half(margin)            # home handicap
p(home)  = Φ(margin / σ)
fair odds= 1/p  and  1/(1−p)                         # no hold applied
total    = Σ_team [ (PF(team) + PA(opp))/2 + ((PF−PA)(team) − (PF−PA)(opp))/2 ]

A second total is computed as (OffRtg + opp DefRtg)/2 × pace/100 per team. The gap between the two is printed per game and any gap over 4.0 pts forces grade C.

5. Grading (not betting)

A = both spread estimators within 1.0 pt, |margin| ≥ 0.75σ, both total estimators within 2.0 pts. B = passes the minimum gates. C = fails one. A grade is a statement about model stability, never about expected profit. Expected profit requires a price, which is why every pick is withheld.

6. Luck

Expected wins come from the fitted model on each team's own point differential; the residual is luck, standardised by sqrt(games·p·(1−p)). Observed: 0 of 30 teams outside |z| ≤ 1.96. That means no team's record in this window is distinguishable from its underlying scoring level.

7. What would change the answer

  • A price (any price) turns a projection into a testable claim.
  • Per-game results turn a testable claim into a score.
  • Player logs turn injuries and props from qualitative notes into numbers.
  • More games narrow σ and h, which are currently identified loosely against a single season.

8. Reproduce it

python3 -m nba2.v2                # rebuilds data/v2/*.json and site/
python3 -m nba2.v2.check          # every row sourced, every blank explained
python3 -m nba2.v2.strategy_lab   # strategies, gates, correlations, luck separation
python3 -m unittest discover -s nba2/v2 -t . -p 'test_*.py'   # 80 tests
