Luck, as a z-score per club

Pythagorean expected wins (exponent 13.91) from officially-sourced points for and against, standardised by each club's own binomial sd. The band is |z| ≤ 2.0; a strategy that leans on a club outside it is refused, not downgraded. Two further exponents are published so a reader can see whether band membership is an artefact of the exponent.
