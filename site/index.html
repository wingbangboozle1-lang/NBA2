2026-11-15, this session: the model's accuracy is not yet separable from "back the better team". A paired test on the 34 games where our model and that naive rule disagreed found the naive rule right 20 times and the model 14 (exact p = 0.3915). The headline walk-forward accuracy is high because favourites win, not because the model adds information. That is published as a finding, with its arithmetic, on Skill vs luck — and it is why no stake is published anywhere on this site.

Read this first. The model can price a game; it cannot price a bet. A bet needs a price to beat, and no odds feed is connected to this build — so the picks page publishes a lean and a trigger price for all 34 games but holds every stake at 0.00 units and prints the gate that did it. Official input facts are captured with dated NBA source links; model outputs are derived from those inputs and labeled accordingly. What this build cannot do — and why that is stated rather than hidden

Blocked inputs
Missing input What it blocks Where it would come from
Market prices (spread, total, moneyline, prop lines) No licensed odds feed is connected and NBA.com renders odds client-side only. Required for: any value/edge claim, stake sizing, ROI, leaderboard scoring. nba.com/games
An automated feed that keeps the results history current without a human capture The 188 official finals from 2026-10-20 to 2026-11-14 are committed under data/raw/, and they rebuild every one of the 30 standings records exactly - including all 30 conference records. What is missing is a scheduled job that can reach nba.com, so the history freezes between manual captures and the window ages. nba.com/games?date=2026-11-14
Per-player season game logs (minutes by game, usage, on/off) nba.com/stats player pages render season aggregates only for this client, and the game-log view returns 'No data available'. Required for: minutes distributions, prop probabilities, and a replacement model that does not depend on one day's game book. nba.com/stats/players/traditional
