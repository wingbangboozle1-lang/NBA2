Originated lines, sourced row by row

A 27-game window (Nov 14 → Nov 17) priced from official NBA data only: two independent ratings, a fitted home-court term, published formulas, and a source link on every row. Every game gets an originated line and a lean; no game gets a stake, because no market price is connected to beat.

Read this first. The model can price a game; it cannot price a bet. A bet needs a price to beat, and no odds feed is connected to this build — so the picks page publishes a lean and a trigger price for all 27 games but holds every stake at 0.00 units and prints the gate that did it. What is genuinely new is verification: every number on this site was re-read from an official NBA page on 2026-11-14, and the identical claims from the previous build were cross-checked against it. Staleness: resolved, and the check that caught it is still running. The standings this build published had been transcribed before the 2026-11-13 finals, so they agreed with the pre-final state on 30 of 30 rows and with live standings on 12 of 30. On 2026-11-14 the table was re-read from nba.com/standings and it now matches live official standings on 30 of 30 rows, home and road splits included. The proof is stronger than a re-read: all 30 records were rebuilt independently from the 183 official final scores in data/raw/history/ and agree exactly. The measure that found the drift is kept, inverted, on the Live verification page.

What this build cannot do — and why that is stated rather than hidden

Blocked inputs
Missing input What it blocks Where it would come from
Market prices (spread, total, moneyline, prop lines) No licensed odds feed is connected and NBA.com renders odds client-side only. Required for: any value/edge claim, stake sizing, ROI, leaderboard scoring. nba.com/games
An automated feed that keeps the results history current without a human capture The 183 official finals from 2026-10-20 to 2026-11-13 ARE committed under data/raw/history/, and they rebuild every one of the 30 standings records exactly. Backtests, out-of-sample splits and settlement therefore run today. What is missing is a scheduled job that appends each night's results, so the history freezes between manual captures and the window ages. nba.com/games?date=2026-11-13
Per-player game logs (minutes, usage, on/off) nba.com/stats player pages render season aggregates only for this client. Required for: minutes distributions, prop bet probabilities, injury replacement modelling. nba.com/stats/players/traditional
Injury designations for game days beyond the next filing deadline The NBA publishes a report only once a team has filed. For a game on day +2 or +3 the filing deadline has not yet passed, so no official row exists to read. official.nba.com/nba-injury-report-2026-27-season/
