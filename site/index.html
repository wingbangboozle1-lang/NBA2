2026-11-15, this session: the model's accuracy is not yet separable from "back the better team". A paired test on the 40 games where our model and that naive rule disagreed found the naive rule right 23 times and the model 17 (exact p = 0.4296). The headline walk-forward accuracy is high because favourites win, not because the model adds information. That is published as a finding, with its arithmetic, on Skill vs luck — and it is why no stake is published anywhere on this site.

Read this first. The model can price a game; it cannot price a bet. A bet needs a price to beat, and no odds feed is connected to this build — so the picks page publishes a lean and a trigger price for all 26 games but holds every stake at 0.00 units and prints the gate that did it. Official input facts are captured with dated NBA source links; model outputs are derived from those inputs and labeled accordingly. The snapshot is not a live refresh, and the latest scheduled ingest failed before rebuilding. See the verification log and Flagged for review.
