What this build cannot do — and why that is stated rather than hidden

Blocked inputs
Missing input What it blocks Where it would come from
Market prices (spread, total, moneyline, prop lines) No licensed odds feed is connected and NBA.com renders odds client-side only. Required for: any value/edge claim, stake sizing, ROI, leaderboard scoring. nba.com/games
An automated feed that keeps the results history current without a human capture The official finals through 2026-11-18 (222 regular-season games, the exact count the official standings card implies) ARE committed: data/raw/history/ through 11/13 plus the nightly-refresh finals feeds finals_2026-11-13..18.json, which rebuild every one of the 30 standings records exactly. The scheduled refresh appends each night's results when the runner transport allows; between its runs the window ages. nba.com/games?date=2026-11-18
Per-player game logs (minutes, usage, on/off) nba.com/stats player pages render season aggregates only for this client. Required for: minutes distributions, prop bet probabilities, injury replacement modelling. The v5 layer captures official gamebook PDFs for the games it covers, which is why player-coverage is windowed rather than season-wide. nba.com/stats/players/traditional
Injury designations for game days beyond the next filing deadline The NBA publishes a report only once a team has filed. For a game on day +2 or +3 the filing deadline has not yet passed, so no official row exists to read. official.nba.com/nba-injury-report-2026-27-season/
