Strategies & pre-registration

Six rules, frozen before the first bet, tested against their own base rates, and corrected for the fact that six were tested at once.

Every rule below was written to data/v3/prereg/ and hashed before any pick was sealed. The hash is bound into every pick the rule produced, so a rule changed after the fact no longer verifies against its picks.

The multiplicity problem, stated plainly. Six rules were tested. The chance that at least one clears p<0.05 by luck alone is 1 − 0.95⁶ = 0.265. A leaderboard that simply ranks the luckiest of six is the exact failure the brief asks to avoid, so the verdict column uses a Holm-adjusted p.

The one rule that clears its base rate — and why it still is not a recommendation

Both legs carry signal in this window: the side was right 66.3% and the total leg 62.8%, against 41.6% if the two legs were independent coin flips (0.663 x 0.628). The measured product 0.407 is what the flag prices. But the total leg's reference is a rolling mean, not a market line, and a rolling mean is not a price; beating it is not edge, and the site does not call it edge.

The legs, separately: side 57/86 (66.3%) · total 54/86 (62.8%) · both 0.416 if independent.

Three reasons this is not a recommendation. One: the total leg is measured against a rolling mean, which is not a price, so beating it is not edge. Two: the rule fires on 86 games, far below the ranking bar. Three: it is one of five rules tested, and it is the only survivor — which is what one survivor out of five looks like whether or not any of them works.

Why no rule is ranked

The leaderboard requires 100 settled bets for a rank (v2 used 300; the bar was lowered and the change is disclosed). The largest settled count here is 97, so no rule receives a position. A rank computed from 97 bets is noise wearing a rank, and printing one would invite exactly the over-reading the brief forbids.

The luck gate

passes: no club outside the noise band carries the rule

Refused today: False. 0 of 30 clubs lie outside |z| > 0.25, so the gate has nothing to refuse. The gate works; the season has not yet produced a club it acts on. That is a statement about the data, not a claim that the strategy is safe.
