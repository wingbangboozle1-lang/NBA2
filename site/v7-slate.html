Every game, both estimators, and the disagreement

Read the two margins first. E1 is the opponent-adjusted rating solved from official finals (a margin, in points). E2 is the unadjusted points-per-game differential. They answer different questions, and the number between them — disagreement — is the confidence signal this page is built around: a large gap means the two views of the same clubs do not agree, and a bet that leans on the blend alone is leaning on an average of a disagreement nobody resolved. The blend is not shown as a headline anywhere on this site.
