The covariance of (margin, total) over settled games. Pull per-game final scores (SG-2) and compute Pearson/Spearman on ≥300 games, per team and league-wide. Q-9 Are the two ratings estimators really independent, or is one a restatement of the other? Measured: r = +0.999, max spread disagreement 0.99 pts across 31 lines. Q-23 When a published snapshot goes stale, should the site keep showing it (labelled) or withhold the page entirely? Proof of the failure mode, measured today: the v2 team table matches the pre-11/13-finals state 30/30 and current official standings only 12/30, while the page still advertises a 30/30 live match. The nine 11/13 finals were played while the table was frozen. A rule. Nothing in the build currently compares a published row against a fresher official capture, so staleness can only be caught by a human reading two pages. Set a maximum snapshot age per table (records: 1 day; prices: 15 minutes; injuries: to the next official filing). Add a staleness gate to nba2/v2/check.py that fails when a row is older than its limit, and render stale rows with an explicit 'as of' badge instead of a MATCH badge. BLOCKING nba.com/standings
Q-24 Is a 4-game, 0-loss forecast worth publishing at all, and what is the minimum n before a strategy may be ranked? The first real out-of-sample result in the repository: nba2/data/picks.json committed 83 minutes before tip, 4 distinct games bet, all 4 won (S1 moneyline and S2 spread fired on the same games, so 8 recorded wins are 4 games). Model called the winner in 5 of 9 games; always-home would have got 7. The promotion threshold. Existing gates want 30 settled bets, which is the right order of magnitude but has never been ratified as a number the owner will accept waiting for. Owner ratifies: minimum n (proposal: 100 settled, to have power to detect a 3-point edge), and whether a 4-0 start is allowed to be displayed at all once n is known to be inadequate. HIGH nba.com/games
Q-25 Should a per-strategy win rate be allowed to count the same game twice when two strategies fire on it? The measured overlap: of 4 graded games, all 4 were bet by both S1_MarginFavorite and S2_SpreadValue. Per-strategy hit rates stay valid; any portfolio or leaderboard return that sums them double-counts the games. A portfolio accounting rule: whether the leaderboard scores strategies independently (as now) or scores the union of positions actually held. Owner decision. Independent scoring is simpler and correct for ranking hypotheses; union scoring is correct for describing what a bankroll would have done. They answer different questions and the site currently only implements one. HIGH nba.com/games

Refresh data/raw with the 30 current records captured today (already in audit/2026-11-14/official_inputs_2026-11-14.json), rebuild, re-seal a new ledger, and compare. Until then no claim about which estimator is better is supported. HIGH nba.com/standings

Questions about the project itself, not the model

Which site is authoritative?

Six trees exist. Until the owner picks one, every published number is ambiguous. Proposed answer: this tree, with the others archived.

Is the reward pool real money?

The brief says rewards for top strategies. The size, funding and legal basis are unknown and change what the leaderboard must be built to prevent.

Who may submit a strategy?

Public submissions turn anti-gaming rules from nice-to-have into load-bearing. One submitter only changes the design substantially.

What counts as a 'pick'?

A projection, a graded bet, or a staked bet? This determines whether anything on this site qualifies today. Current answer: nothing does.

Information we do not have, that someone must go and get

  • Licensed odds history (opening and closing lines) — the only way to measure edge.
  • Per-game box scores — the only way to settle, backtest or score.
  • Player game logs with minutes — the only way to price props or quantify injuries.
  • Play-by-play — the only way to analyse game flow rather than schedule density.
  • An explicit statement of what the reward is, so the leaderboard can be designed against it.
