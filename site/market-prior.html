Market-anchored prior

Blend the model line with the closing line, weighted by measured out-of-sample log-loss. The rule is pre-registered and published; the weight is blank, because no closing line has ever been captured.

The measured model is currently worse than a coin flip on the settled sample. Log-loss 0.7058 against a baseline of 0.6931 — skill 0.0. That is a result, not a bug, and it is published rather than gated away: F-F5-08. The immediate consequence for this page is that the pre-registered rule gives the entire weight to the market as soon as the market shows any skill at all, because the model has shown none on these games. It also means the honest blended line right now is the market line — which is precisely the thing this repository cannot see.
The brief asks to blend the model line with the closing line, weighted by measured out-of-sample log-loss. Only one of the two inputs exists. No closing price has ever been captured (R-043 / R-101, F-V4-04), so the market's log-loss cannot be measured, so the weight is undefined. It is published as a blank with that reason — not as 0, not as 0.5, and not as a plausible guess.

The rule, pre-registered before any price exists

w_market = max(0, L0 − L_market) / (max(0, L0 − L_model) + max(0, L0 − L_market)); blended = (1 − w_market)·model + w_market·market

L0 = ln 2 = 0.6931 nats  (a coin flip on a binary outcome)
s = L0 − L, floored at 0   (a forecaster worse than a coin flip gets no weight,
                            never negative weight)
Pre-registering the rule now is the point: it fixes the weight function before there is a market number to fit it to, so the blend cannot later be reverse-engineered from a result.

What the rule would return, for every plausible market log-loss

Every row is the pre-registered rule evaluated at a hypothetical market log-loss. It is arithmetic on the stated formula, not a measurement: no market log-loss has been observed, because no closing price exists in any committed capture.
Because the model currently shows no skill against a coin flip on the settled sample, the rule gives the market the entire weight for any market log-loss below the baseline. That is the rule working as pre-registered, and it is a statement about the sample (22 graded games), not a proof about the model.

What would unblock it

  1. A licensed odds feed capturing the closing spread, total and moneyline per game with a stated timestamp policy (R-101).
  2. A decision on storage and display terms for those prices (open question on the Decisions needed page).
  3. At least one full out-of-sample window of market log-loss scored on the same games as the model, so the two numbers are comparable.
