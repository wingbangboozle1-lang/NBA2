D-01 BLOCKING Market price Which licensed odds source do we measure against, and may we store its closing prices? Nothing. No price of any kind exists in the repository. Confirmed again this session: no odds field in any committed snapshot, and every pick publishes '0.00 u' with gate G6 named. A licensed feed (opening + closing), its terms of use for storage, and the exact timestamp policy (which price is 'the' price). Pick a vendor, obtain written confirmation that historical storage is permitted, then add one ingest path writing dated snapshots. Until then every ROI/CLV/edge figure must stay blank. Decides whether this is a betting system at all. Without a price, every output is a projection and the leaderboard cannot rank on money. D-02 BLOCKING Player-level data Do we obtain a per-game player box-score feed, and if so which one? Team-level totals only. Every player-level table on this site is blank by policy, and the three player correlations are marked BLOCKED. Per-game player lines with minutes, so props, prop combos, same-player correlation and the minutes half of injury impact can be computed at all. A stats vendor, or the league's stats endpoints if the licence permits. Check the retention clause before committing data to the repository. D-03 BLOCKING Evidence standard Is the 300-settled-bet bar (gate G7) the promotion standard, or should it be replaced by a per-strategy power calculation? A flat 300-bet bar is registered. The largest strategy here has 167 settled bets. A paired discordant-game test (this session) needs roughly an order of magnitude fewer games to detect the same effect. An explicit choice of evidence standard, and the false-positive rate the owner is willing to accept across a leaderboard of N strategies. Decide the effect size worth detecting (e.g. 55% vs 50%), then compute the required n per strategy; publish it next to each strategy instead of one global bar. Decides when — if ever — a reward is paid. Also decides whether the competition will be won by luck: testing many strategies without a multiplicity correction guarantees a false winner. Reward allocation, leaderboard ranking, the meaning of 'passed all gates'. Owner OPEN github.com/wingbangboozle1-lang/NBA2/blob/main/nba2…
D-04 HIGH Competition design Who may submit strategies, and is the reward real money, a score, or neither? A leaderboard design with anti-gaming rules and an unfunded 100-unit pool exists as a proposal. Whether submissions are public; whether the reward has monetary value; the jurisdiction and legal basis if it does. Answer the three sub-questions in order; they cascade. If the pool is real money, the anti-gaming rules, audit trail and (in the Netherlands) the Kansspelautoriteit rules all become load-bearing before launch. Changes the entire anti-gaming design. A solo, unranked research desk needs none of it. Leaderboard promotion, reward rules, submission review workflow. Owner OPEN kansspelautoriteit.nl/
D-05 HIGH Model evidence Given that the model's accuracy is not separable from 'back the better team' (p = 0.30 on 33 discordant games), what would count as enough evidence of skill to publish a stake? A paired McNemar test on 33 discordant games: naive rule 20, model 13, p = 0.2962. Wilson intervals on every rate. A 167-game walk-forward with no look-ahead. A pre-registered decision rule: which test, on which games, with what threshold, decided before the next slate is graded. Pre-register the discordant-game test now (it is already implemented), fix the significance threshold and the multiplicity correction, then let the nightly settlement accumulate games. Do not change the rule once results arrive. Determines whether the model can ever be said to add information beyond the standings. Also protects against the most common failure: choosing the test after seeing the answer. Any claim of skill; any stake above zero; S1..S6 promotion. Owner OPEN github.com/wingbangboozle1-lang/NBA2/blob/main/site…

D-07 MEDIUM Rotation minutes Is there a defensible way to convert 'player X is Out' into a points adjustment without player-level data? Official statuses and reasons for every game in the window, refreshed from the official PDF. No minutes data. Per-player minutes and usage, and a measured relationship between a player's absence and team scoring. Either obtain player box scores (D-02) and measure the historical on/off effect, or state explicitly that no adjustment is made and that the model is availability-blind. The second is honest and free; the first is the real fix. Right now injuries are tracked accurately but not priced. A reader must not infer that they are in the number. The injury-to-production half of the brief; any 'injury-adjusted line'. Owner OPEN official.nba.com/nba-injury-report-2026-27-season/

No play-by-play. Play-by-play data for in-game flow (runs, pace changes, garbage time), or a decision that schedule-spot analysis is what was meant. A one-line answer settles the scope. If in-game flow is meant, a play-by-play feed is required and should be added to D-02's scope. Decides whether the existing game-flow page is complete or a placeholder. Nothing.
