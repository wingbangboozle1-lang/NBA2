Strategy lab

Seven candidate strategies, each scored walk-forward over 167 predictions built from the 183 official finals in this repository (2026-10-23 → 2026-11-13). Twelve validation gates, each evaluated from the data rather than asserted. Then the part that matters most: how much of every record is luck.

Nothing on this page is a bet, and nothing is ranked. Two gates fail outright: G6, because no market price is connected, and G7, because the largest settled sample here is 167 bets against a 300-bet bar. Until those pass, the leaderboard is a scoring exercise, not a competition, and the reward pool stays unfunded. That is a deliberate design choice: a leaderboard that can be topped by a lucky 7-0 run is worse than no leaderboard.

How to read this. At the measured rho of +0.0393 the joint is close to the product, but the 95% interval on rho runs -0.1133 to +0.1900 — wide enough that the honest statement is 'we do not know this correlation precisely enough to price a same-game parlay', not 'the legs are independent'. A two-leg price quoted from the product is therefore a guess with a known error bar, and the site withholds it rather than print the guess.

4. Luck versus skill

The trap on this table. S4 is 7 for 7 — a 100% record, and a binomial p-value of 0.016 that would pass a naive significance test. It is still not evidence of skill: at seven bets the interval is wide enough to hold almost any true rate, which is exactly why the verdict column reads "samples too small to call" and why the leaderboard refuses to rank it. A record this short is what luck looks like when it is winning.

Scoring metric Log-loss on settled bets, not win rate Win rate rewards luck on small samples; log-loss is a proper scoring rule, so honest probabilities beat lucky ones. PROPOSED nba.com/standings

6. What would move a strategy onto the board

  1. A dated market price (G6). One odds feed with timestamps turns every projection on this site into a testable claim. Nothing else on this list matters as much.
  2. More settled bets (G7). The bar is 300. The largest sample here is 167, and the season produces roughly 12 games a day, so the bar is reachable inside a season rather than in years.
  3. Per-player game logs. Unblocks S7 and every prop-combo correlation on the correlations table. These are the only correlations on this site still listed as blocked for lack of data rather than lack of power.
