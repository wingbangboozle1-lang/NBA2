The redistribution rule

Top-8 shares = mpg / Σ(top-8 mpg). Replacement shares: each out player's mpg is redistributed to the remaining players proportionally to their own mpg (new_mpg_j = mpg_j + Σ_out mpg_i × mpg_j / Σ_avail mpg_k). Mechanical rule, no fit. It runs the moment the remaining players' logs are captured; until then the receiving column is blank and flagged.

This session captured the players who are out. The receiving side needs the players who are in, so the rule is published with its denominator empty rather than filled with a proxy. nba2/v4/minutes.py::redistribution() runs it the moment those logs land.
