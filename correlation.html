Correlation Values
Correlation between parlay legs and player-prop combo legs is determined and applied so multi-leg tickets are priced correctly. All correlation values are blank + unverified until calculated from verified data.
Rule: correlation values are never inferred. A blank value means the correlation has not been determined from verified data. Parlays and prop combos cannot pass validation gate GATE-04 until their correlation values are populated and sourced.

How correlation is determined (method, not values)

  • Use verified official NBA.com player/team stats as the data input.
  • Compute the Pearson (or Spearman) correlation between the two legs over a historical window.
  • Adjust the parlay implied probability by the correlation; do not treat legs as independent when they are not.
  • Record the coefficient, the window, and the source link; flag unverified if any input is blank.
  • Only populated correlation values may be used in validation gate GATE-04.
