Leaderboard competition

Strategies are the entrants. The leaderboard ranks them on risk-adjusted, luck-discounted performance and pays rewards to the top tier — designed so that only demonstrated skill (not variance) can win. The full design below is a proposal awaiting your sign-off via the Suggestions workflow before any scoring goes live.

Leaderboard state: pre-launch. Scoring requires settled, gate-passed bets, which require (a) odds/results feeds and (b) an activated strategy. Both are pending — open questions Q-B1…Q-B5 decide launch parameters.
