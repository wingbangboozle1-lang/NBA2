Correlation

Combos (parlays, same-game player-prop combos) live or die on correlation. This page defines the math we use, the market conventions books apply, the caps we enforce, and — critically — the honest state of our own correlation estimates: none exist yet, so the matrix ships as a template with placeholder values that are never used until replaced by data-derived coefficients.

Market convention: books price correlation deliberately

ConventionDocumented behaviorSource
Correlated legs banned in standard parlaysParlays may not combine related outcomes unless explicitly offered as a Same Game Parlay product; violators may be voided or settled as singles.FanDuel house rules (PA/OH)
Related outcomes voided if accepted in errorBooks reserve the right to void parlay parts that include related contingencies not priced for the relationship.DraftKings betting rules (Golden Nugget retail)
SGPs may be restricted "due to correlation"Books may limit or suspend SGP offerings on specific games/markets precisely because of correlation risk.BetSaracen house rules (Aug 2026)
Push recalculationParlay legs that push/no-action reduce the parlay to remaining legs (relevant for spreads/totals on whole numbers).BetSaracen house rules · FanDuel
Implication: the market already charges for correlation in SGPs. Our edge must come from pricing the correlation better than the book does — never from pretending it isn't there. That is gate G12.
