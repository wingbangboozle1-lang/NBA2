Strategies

The line-origination pipeline, then the 12-strategy catalog across single bets, multi-game parlays, and same-game player-prop combos. Every strategy is pre-registered in data/strategies_lab.json — its signal, its gates, and its activation status. Nothing generates a real pick until all of its gates pass out-of-sample.

How a line is originated (order matters)

StepInputOutputRule / source
1. Base ratingsTeam efficiencies and minutes-weighted lineups from completed games.Offensive/defensive ratings per team per game context.Internal method — defined in Game Flow; requires data feed (see I-2)
2. Availability passOfficial injury report (5 p.m. local the day before; 1 p.m. local for B2B game day; continuous updates).Projected rotation minutes per player; usage redistribution.official.nba.com injury report policy · framework
3. Environment passSchedule spot (B2B, 3-in-4, travel), rest rules, clutch/garbage-time tendencies.Pace/efficiency adjustments.official schedule · PPP rest rules
4. Originate linesRatings × minutes × environment.Fair spread, total, moneyline, and player-prop numbers (PRA etc.).Internal method — no market anchor: lines are fixed before any market number is viewed (data/strategies_lab.json → global_rules)
5. Record market contextMarket lines (only after origination).Edge = our fair probability − breakeven probability; CLV measured later at close.Dare/Dennis/Paul (2015): closing line is fair; edge must be captured early — CLV gate

What graduates a strategy to ACTIVE?

Only the gate engine: every listed gate for that strategy must pass on out-of-sample data, with the evidence recorded in tools/generate_picks_lab.py's BACKTEST_EVIDENCE table (currently empty by design). See Validation Gates and the open questions (backtest infrastructure and historical-odds archive are unresolved decisions).
