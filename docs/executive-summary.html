Executive summary

Where the project stands, what has been verified, what is deliberately missing, and exactly what must happen before real picks can be generated. Details live on the linked subpages.

1 · Mission

Originate our own NBA lines; develop selectively skill-based strategies (single bets, parlays, player-prop combos); force each strategy through validation gates that strip out luck; measure correlation and cap it; then generate picks for every upcoming slate up to a maximum of 3 days in advance. A leaderboard competition ranks strategies and rewards top performers. Injury/rotation impacts and game-flow environments are tracked as first-class inputs.

4 · The framework in eight steps

  1. Originate: power ratings + schedule spots + injury-adjusted rotation projections → fair spread, total, moneyline and prop numbers, produced before seeing any market line (method).
  2. Signal: 12 pre-registered strategies convert gaps between our lines and reality into candidate bets (catalog).
  3. Gate: each candidate must pass G1–G13 on out-of-sample data — sample size, ROI, positive CLV, calibration, walk-forward, drawdown, significance, no cherry-picking, regime checks (gates).
  4. De-luck: luck is removed statistically, not anecdotally — binomial and t-tests, bootstrap confidence intervals, Brier calibration, and CLV as a forward-looking skill proxy (method).
  5. Correlate: combos are repriced with joint probabilities (P1·P2 + covariance) and capped in the portfolio; nothing correlated is ever bet at unadjusted prices (math).
  6. Generate: picks for slates ≤3 days ahead via tools/generate_picks_lab.py, which refuses to emit anything from a strategy that hasn't passed its gates (pipeline).
  7. Stake: quarter-Kelly, capped (≤2% per single, ≤1% per combo leg-group; portfolio exposure ≤12%) (policy).
  8. Compete & reward: strategies are leaderboard entrants scored on profit + CLV + calibration − drawdown, with promotion/relegation tiers and a reward pool (design).

6 · Bottom line

The system is real, the picks are not — yet, by design. Every mechanical component exists and runs; the two things that unlock real picks are (a) data feeds and (b) at least one strategy passing all gates out-of-sample. Until then the generator stays blocked, and this is the correct, skill-preserving behavior — betting before proving an edge is how luck masquerades as skill.
