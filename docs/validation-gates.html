Validation gates

Every strategy must pass every gate listed for it, on out-of-sample data, before it may generate a single pick. The gates exist to remove luck from the equation: variance makes any short hot streak indistinguishable from skill, so the acceptance criteria are statistical, not anecdotal.

Current status: 0 / 13 gates have passing evidence. No backtests have been run (no historical odds archive or data feed is connected — I-2). The pick generator enforces this: BACKTEST_EVIDENCE is empty by design, so every strategy is blocked from real picks. See the enforcement code.

The gates

GateDefinition / thresholdWhat it protects againstBasis
G1 — Minimum samplen ≥ 500 bets (spreads/totals), n ≥ 300 (props/combos), n ≥ 200 (low-frequency spots) in backtest.Small-sample noise; a 12–5 stretch is meaningless at n=17.Internal; binomial variance math (derivable) — per-strategy gate lists in strategies.json
G2 — Positive ROIROI > 0 after commission (standard -110 ⇒ breakeven rate 110/210 ≈ 52.38%; derived arithmetic — math).Strategies that win often but lose money.market-commission convention (Compton & Sigler)
G3 — Positive closing-line valueMean CLV > 0 over the sample: our taken price must beat the closing price on average.Being right for the wrong reason; CLV is the forward-looking skill proxy because the close is fair (Dare/Dennis/Paul 2015).Dare/Dennis/Paul (2015)
G4 — CalibrationBrier score of our fair probabilities must beat the market's Brier score on the same events, and predicted win rates must match observed rates within ±3 pts per decile.Confident-but-wrong probabilities (mispriced edges compound into mis-sized stakes).Internal; Brier (1950) scoring is standard probability practice (derivable method) — dashboard proposal P-4
G5 — Walk-forwardAll evidence must be walk-forward (train on past, bet next game only, roll forward). No in-sample declarations.Look-ahead bias and overfitting.Internal method — pre-registered in strategies.json
G6 — Drawdown limitMax historical drawdown ≤ 25% of bankroll under quarter-Kelly staking; 10% live kill-switch suspends the strategy pending review.Ruin risk; Kelly grows fast when variance is underestimated.Kelly (1956) basis; thresholds internal policy
G7 — Significancet-stat ≥ 2.0 on per-bet returns (≈ p<0.05) AND binomial test on cover rate vs. breakeven rate rejects fairness at 95%.Luck: at 52.38% breakeven, even a true 55% bettor needs hundreds of bets for 95% confidence.Standard statistics (derivable); sample burden consistent with 10k-game market studies
G8 — No cherry-pickingRules fixed and pre-registered before the test window; parameter changes reset the backtest clock; the ledger of every historical pick is immutable.Backtesting to a chosen rule-set (the main way luck is laundered into "skill").Internal method; enforced via pre-registration file
G9 — Regime checkEdge must persist across seasons, months, and both conferences; not dependent on one roster era or rule change.Regime-dependent artifacts (e.g., pre-PPP rest patterns).Internal; regime context from PPP (2023)
G10 — Portfolio capPositions per slate: ≤8 singles, ≤2 parlays, ≤2 SGPs; total exposure ≤12% bankroll; pairwise |corr| ≤ 0.35.Concentration risk making a good strategy blow up.correlation caps
G11 — Correlation matrix requiredCombo strategies may not trade until the matrix in data/demo/correlation.demo.csv is replaced with coefficients estimated from historical co-occurrence data.Guessing covariance → mispriced joint probability → mispriced SGP edge.estimation method
G12 — SGP repriceSame-game combos must be repriced by us (joint probability incl. covariance) and only bet when edge ≥ 2 pts over offered implied joint probability.Books already price correlation into SGPs (house rules); ignoring it is negative-EV.FanDuel house rules · DraftKings rules
G13 — Parlay haircutMulti-game parlay EV must exceed the best single-leg EV after subtracting a documented correlation/compounding haircut.Parlays amplify variance and book margin; must clear a higher bar than singles.compounding math

How luck is removed (and skill proven) — four independent tests

TestWhat it showsPass conditionNotes
Cover-rate binomial testWhether win rate exceeds the commission breakeven rate (52.38% at -110) more than chance allows.Reject "fair coin at breakeven" at 95% given n (G7).Requires n ≈ 600+ for a true 55% edge to be distinguishable at 95% — computed via standard binomial power (derivable arithmetic; tool computes exact n)
CLV testWhether we beat the closing line on average — the market's fairest price (biases removed by close).Mean CLV > 0 with t-stat ≥ 2.0 (G3+G7).Closing-line fairness: Dare/Dennis/Paul (2015)
Calibration / BrierWhether our stated probabilities are honest, not just profitable in one sample.Brier better than market's; decile errors ≤ ±3 pts (G4).Internal standard method — see P-4 dashboard
Bootstrap drawdownResample bet sequence 10,000× to estimate the distribution of future drawdowns under the staking policy.95th-percentile drawdown ≤ 25% (G6).Internal standard method; Kelly stakes per Kelly (1956)

Acceptance workflow

  1. Strategy defined and frozen in data/strategies_lab.json (pre-registration = G8).
  2. Backtest harness runs walk-forward (G5) on historical odds archive — needs the archive decision first (Q-B3).
  3. Evidence appended to BACKTEST_EVIDENCE in tools/generate_picks_lab.py with dates, n, ROI, CLV, t-stat, Brier, drawdown.
  4. Independent re-run by a different session re-computes the numbers (four-eyes principle, logged in COORDINATION.md).
  5. Only then does the strategy flip to active, and the generator starts emitting picks for it.
