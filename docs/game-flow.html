Game flow analysis

"Game flow" is how a game's environment — pace, clock structure, clutch time, blowouts, late fouling, rest — changes the distribution of scoring and minutes. It determines not just spreads and totals but the shape of player-prop outcomes. Below: the verified structural facts, and the framework for turning them into line adjustments.
