Suggestions — review before anything changes

A dedicated review section: every proposed improvement to the system or this site is listed here with benefit, cost, and risk. Nothing is implemented until you pick a path. Set a proposal's status to Approved / Rejected / Needs-info; unreviewed proposals stay Proposed and dormant.

How to submit a new suggestion

  1. Open an issue in the repo (Issues) titled SUGGESTION: <title>, or edit this page directly on your branch.
  2. State: the decision it improves, benefit, effort (S/M/L), risk, and any dependency on Section B.
  3. A session will add it to the backlog with status PROPOSED; it stays dormant until you change the status.
