NBA line origination, verified from the ground up

This project originates its own NBA lines, develops signal-based strategies across single bets, parlays and player-prop combos, and only lets strategies that pass every validation gate generate picks — for slates up to three days ahead. Nothing on this site is asserted without a source, and values that cannot be confirmed from an official source are left blank and flagged.

Scope & legality notice. This site is research documentation, not betting advice. The project owner is in the Netherlands: online gambling is legal there only on operators licensed by the Kansspelautoriteit (KSA), for players 18+, with the Kansspelwijzer register as the authoritative licence check and CRUKS as the national self-exclusion register. What this system does

  • Originates its own lines first — power ratings, schedule spots, and injury-adjusted rotation projections produce fair spread / total / prop numbers before any market line is looked at (anchoring prevention rule). Method
  • Runs 12 pre-registered strategies across single bets, multi-game parlays and same-game player-prop combos, each with its own signal and gate list. Catalog
  • Removes luck from evaluation — sample-size minimums, positive CLV, calibration, walk-forward out-of-sample testing and significance tests decide what graduates; a hot streak alone never does. Gates
  • Prices correlation explicitly — no combo is bet without our own joint-probability repricing and a portfolio correlation cap. Correlation
  • Generates slate picks up to 3 days ahead from the originated lines through a runnable pipeline that refuses to emit picks until gates pass. Picks
  • Tracks injuries → rotation minutes → production and game-flow environments on the league's official reporting clock. Injuries · Game flow
  • Complements parallel sessions' work — the root dashboard, the verified 3-day slate (31 games, Nov 13–16) and the official injury snapshot (11/13 report rows) are cross-linked here, not duplicated.

Verification protocol (how to read every table here)

  • Every table row carries a source link. Factual rows link official or documented sources; internal methodology rows link to the section of this site that defines them.
  • A value that could not be confirmed from an official source is left blank and marked UNVERIFIED. Values are never inferred.
  • Demo/synthetic values are marked DEMO and never mixed into factual tables.
  • The repo tool tools/verify_site.py enforces these rules mechanically (row-has-link, no broken links, no placeholders) and the build fails if they break.
