Section A — answered from information we already have (verified)

Q-IDQuestionAnswerSource
Q-A1Is this legal where the owner is located?NL: online gambling is legal only on KSA-licensed operators (market opened Oct 1, 2021); 18+; CRUKS exclusion register; the Kansspelwijzer register is the authoritative licence check. Decision required: bet only via licensed operators after checking the register.KSA (official) · legal summary
Q-A2When exactly is injury information available?5 p.m. local the day before (non-B2B); 1 p.m. local for B2B second games; game-day updates 11 a.m.–1 p.m. local (8–10 a.m. early tips); continuous updates.official.nba.com
Q-A3Can rest be predicted?Partially: PPP constrains star rest (max 1 star/game, national TV and Cup games protected, home/road balance); pre-approved B2B rest exists for 35+, 34,000 min, or 1,000 games; 65-game rule shapes late-season availability incentives.NBA.com PPP · FTW · The Athletic
Q-A4Are NBA betting markets beatable?Mostly efficient: closing lines are fair (biases at open are corrected by close). Edge exists in the open→close window and specific spots (e.g., home underdogs ≥10 historically), and must be re-validated continuously.Dare/Dennis/Paul (2015) · Compton & Sigler
Q-A5Do books price correlation?Yes — correlated legs are banned in standard parlays; SGPs are a repriced product; books may void or restrict correlated offerings.FanDuel · DraftKings · BetSaracen
Q-A6How should stakes be sized?Fractional Kelly (quarter) with caps; full Kelly is growth-optimal but high-variance. Policy defined at staking.Kelly (1956)
Q-A7Where does official data come from?Sportradar is the exclusive global distributor of official NBA betting data (8-year deal from 2023-24); NBA platform terms prohibit unauthorized scraping, so acquisition must be licensed.Sportico · NBA platform terms
Q-A8What's the current competitive calendar?2026-27 season live since Oct 20, 2026; Cup group play Oct 30–Nov 27; Christmas slate Dec 25; All-Star Feb 19–21, 2027 (Phoenix); season ends April 11, 2027.NBA.com schedule release
Q-A9What did the repo contain before this work?Only a one-line README ("# NBA2") — nothing to review or reuse; everything here is newly built and documented.initial commit 623b4c2
